# v0.1.0 in a browser

The Web export of build f847ef2 (`scripts/export-web.sh`, the merged `build/v0.1.0` tip with every Tenant that landed: the atlas in Map, the collection page in Collection, the Muse title on Phone) driven in headless Chrome (software WebGL) through the DevTools protocol by `modules/shell/playtest/browser_play.py` with real mouse events, at three browser windows: `1920x1080/`, `1440x900/` (the game's minimum) and `1440x400/` (shorter than the minimum, on purpose).

One run per folder, the same gestures: `w01-launch` Collection active, sixteen cards; `w02-map` a click on the Map tab, the atlas window on its page; `w03-map-zoomed` three wheel clicks up at the page centre (1.0x to 1.6x); `w04-collection` a click on the Collection tab, the same sixteen cards; `w05-has-image` a click on Has image (10 of 16); `w06-after-card` a click on the first card (identical pixels: the click only emits `card_selected`); `w07-grow-0..5` the stub's Blank Page growing in; `w08-blank-page` the seventh tab with its close button; `w09-after-close` after closing it, six tabs again. `sheet-bar.png` is the toolbar band of the settled screenshots, stacked. `1920x1080/zoom-*.png` are 4x crops: a tab join, the bar's bottom line, the six titles, the stub and the Home icon.

What the pictures show: the bar is one continuous line across the window at every width; the diagonal joins between tabs are a light grey stair-stepped line with no gaps; the six Muse titles render, and at six tabs the row is narrow enough that every title but Map is cut with a dotted ellipsis (Sket…, 3D V…, Vide…, Colle…, Pho…); the Phone tab now carries its title (#34); the atlas renders its sea, land, region tags and bitmap city names in the browser and the wheel zooms it; the card grid lays out six per row at 1920 and four at 1440 with the Info box over its foot; nothing tells the active tab from the others (#45).

Wrong at 1440x400: the game's window minimum is 1440x900 and the browser cannot honour it, so Godot renders a 900-tall frame into a 400-tall canvas and the browser shows its bottom 400 rows — the bar is off the top of the screen while clicks still land in the bar's coordinates (`1440x400/w01-launch.png` equals rows 500..900 of `1440x900/w01-launch.png` to the pixel). Filed as #46; nothing in this run fixes it.
