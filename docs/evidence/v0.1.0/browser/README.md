# v0.1.0 in a browser

The Web export of build d59e62e (`scripts/export-web.sh`; branch `wip/shell-bottom` on `build/v0.1.0`: the bar along the bottom of the window, seven fixed tabs, the launch grow and the tab-switch cross-fade, the atlas in Map, the collection page in Collection, the two draft mockups in Playground and Phone) driven in headless Chrome (software WebGL) through the DevTools protocol by `modules/shell/playtest/browser_play.py` with real mouse events, at three browser windows: `1920x1080/`, `1440x900/` (the game's minimum) and `1440x400/` (shorter than the minimum, on purpose).

One run per folder, the same gestures: `w01-launch` Collection active after its launch grow and fade, sixteen cards; `w02-switch-0..3` frames caught right after a click on the Map tab (the dip, the cross-fade); `w02-map` the atlas window on its page; `w03-map-zoomed` three wheel clicks up at the page centre (1.0x to 1.6x); `w04-collection` a click on the Collection tab, the same sixteen cards; `w05-has-image` a click on Has image (10 of 16); `w06-after-card` a click on the first card (identical pixels: the click only emits `card_selected`); `w07-playground` the Playground mockup — the owner's Digital Playground reference at native size, centred; `w08-phone` the Phone mockup — the Nokia handset at native size, centred; `w09-grow-0..5` the stub's Blank Page growing in; `w10-blank-page` the eighth tab with its close button; `w11-after-close` after closing it, seven tabs again. `sheet-bar.png` is the toolbar band (now the bottom of the window) of the settled screenshots, stacked.

What the pictures show: the bar is one continuous line along the bottom of the window at every width, with the page filling the space above it; the seven Muse titles render (Map, Sketchbook, 3D Viewer, Video Player, Collection, Playground, Phone), every one cut with the dotted ellipsis at seven-tab width (M…, Sk…, 3D…, Vi…, Cc…, Pla…, Ph…); the atlas renders and the wheel zooms it; the card grid lays out six per row at 1920 and four at 1440 with the Info box just above the bar; the two mockups sit centred at native size; nothing tells the active tab from the others (#45).

Wrong at 1440x400: the game's window minimum is 1440x900 and the browser cannot honour it, so Godot renders a 900-tall frame into a 400-tall canvas and the browser shows its bottom 400 rows — with the bar now along the bottom, the bar itself is what shows, and the page is off the top of the screen while clicks still land in the 900-tall frame's coordinates. Filed as #46; nothing in this run fixes it.
