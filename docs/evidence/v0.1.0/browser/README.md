# v0.1.0 in a browser

The Web export of build 10bea97 (`scripts/export-web.sh`; `build/v0.1.0` after the fold of `wip/shell-bottom`, `wip/atlas-desktop`, `wip/collection-desktop`, `wip/video` and `wip/sculpture`: the bar along the bottom of the window, seven fixed tabs, every tab's Tenant registered) driven in headless Chrome (software WebGL) through the DevTools protocol by `modules/shell/playtest/browser_play.py` with real mouse events, at `1920x1080/` and `1440x900/` (the game's minimum). The click targets come from the native playtests' logged rects (`docs/evidence/*/report.json`), re-fitted to the window by each desktop's own fit rule. `1440x400/` is the older run of build d59e62e kept as the wrong-on-purpose case (#46).

One run per folder, the same gestures: `w01-launch` Collection active after its launch grow and fade — prototype-81's Image Viewer desktop; `w02-switch-0..3` frames caught right after a click on the Map tab (the dip, the cross-fade); `w02-map` the atlas desktop — minimap, itinerary, chat, the World map window, the notification; `w03-map-zoomed` three wheel clicks up at the map body's centre (1.0x to 1.6x); `w04-sketchbook` the Sketchbook tab, the book open on its window; `w05-sketchbook-drawn` a 90x40 px drag across the left page — one ink stroke, the pencil cursor at its end; `w06-3d-viewer` the 3D Viewer tab — the catalogue window and the viewer window with the Buddha scan orbiting; `w07-3d-viewer-orbited` after a 120x40 px drag inside the viewport (the scan turned); `w08-video` the Video Player tab — the Fly Through viewer with the first video playing (The Observer, 00:03); `w09-video-tile-3` after a click on the third tile (A Look Into Our Collection's Polaroids by Andy…, from 0:00); `w10-collection` back on Collection, unchanged; `w11-collection-dragged` a 160x120 px drag on the equipment window's title bar — it moves and comes to the top, over the Image Viewer; `w12-playground` the Playground mockup; `w13-phone` the Phone mockup; `w14-grow-0..5` the stub's Blank Page growing in; `w15-blank-page` the eighth tab with its close button; `w16-after-close` after closing it, seven tabs again, Phone active. `sheet-bar.png` is the toolbar band of the settled screenshots, stacked; `sheet-tabs.png` is one settled screenshot per tab, at a third.

What the pictures show: every one of the seven tabs holds its Tenant in the browser and answers the mouse — the map zooms, the stroke lands, the scan orbits, the tile loads its video, the desktop window drags and stacks; the bar is one continuous line along the bottom at both widths, the seven Muse titles cut with the dotted ellipsis at seven-tab width (M…, Sk…, 3D…, Vi…, Cc…, Pla…, Ph…) and to one glyph with the eighth tab open; nothing tells the active tab from the others (#45); at 1440x900 the Image Viewer body shows two artworks and cuts the caption row, at 1920x1080 three (the reference shows seven in two rows — the owner picks the card scale); the sketchbook desktop is the prototype's, so the book sits at the lower right of the page with white around it; the atlas toolbar shortens its labels at 1440 (Jump to, Sheet).

Wrong before this run, fixed in 10bea97: the Video Player tab was white in the browser (build 961da35) — `FileAccess.file_exists` is false for an imported PNG in an export, so the Tenant's `create()` said `asset_missing`; it now checks the PNG with `ResourceLoader.exists`.
