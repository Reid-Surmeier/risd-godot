# tab_strip playtest evidence

Produced by `scripts/playtest.sh tab_strip` (real mouse events through Godot's input pipeline on Godot 4.7.2, X display), verified by `modules/tab_strip/playtest/verify.py`, which re-hashes every screenshot and checks the logged states against the interface contract. `verify.json` is the verdict (36/36 pass at build c12aa1d: the 29 accepted checks plus the fixed-tab segment of #39, screenshots 13-15); `report.json` is the harness log with timestamps; `grow.mp4` is every rendered frame of the open gesture, `close.mp4` every frame of a close (fold into a stub, then the row slides shut); `grow-frames.png` the first 24 of them; `sheet-bar.png` the toolbar band of the numbered screenshots.

**Issue #45, the active tab tint.** The active tab's face now carries the atlas world map's sea blue `#83e5f7` (the ocean of `modules/atlas/assets/terrain.png`) at 12 percent, so a plain white face reads `(240, 252, 254)` against the inactive tabs' `(255, 255, 255)`. It fades in over 0.2 s when a tab becomes active and out on the tab it leaves: a click, `select_tab`, a new tab settling, the neighbour after a close. The numbered screenshots are regenerated with it (44/44 checks; the eight new ones are the `active_*`, `tint_fades_over_200ms_*` and `mid_fade_frame_*` checks, and `first_tab_pixels_identical` now compares 01 with 05, where the first tab is active in both). `active-tab-2x.png` is two tabs from 06 at 2x (Blank Page active); `fade-sheet.png` is every rendered frame after the click on tab 1 with the tints the strip reported. The harness films each selection (`select-0-frames/`, `select-1-frames/`, `select-fixed-frames/` in a run's out-dir, next to `frames/` and `close-frames/`) and logs every frame's tints and process delta; verify.py times the fade in process time, since the PNG saves stall wall time. The tint multiplies every piece of the tab, because the icon and label pixels are opaque on white: dark glyphs stay dark, and the grey outline moves a few levels (`133,135,137` to `125,133,137`).

`browser/` predates #45 (no tint) and was not re-run. `browser/` — the Web export driven in headless Chrome (software WebGL) at a 1440 px window through the DevTools protocol (`modules/tab_strip/playtest/browser_play.py`): real mouse clicks on the stub and tabs, screenshots of what a browser actually renders. `web-sheet-zoom.png` is the toolbar band across the run; `spot-join.png` and `spot-line.png` are the two places the owner flagged, at 8x.
