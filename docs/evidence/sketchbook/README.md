# sketchbook playtest evidence (build/v0.1.0, the native sketchbook as the Sketchbook Tenant)

Produced by `scripts/playtest.sh sketchbook` on Godot 4.7.2 (X display :99, OpenGL — Mesa's OpenGL ES fallback on this WSL host, so software-rendered — window 1920x1080 then 1440x900). The harness (`modules/sketchbook/playtest/harness.gd`, on `testing/harness_base.gd`) builds the Shell with the book's desktop in the Sketchbook Tab and nothing in the other Tabs, drives it with real mouse events through `Input.parse_input_event`, and reaches the book only through `ShellInterface.tenant_state`. Verified by `modules/sketchbook/playtest/verify.py`, which re-hashes every screenshot, re-reads pixels (tldraw's ink blue inside the page rect) and checks the logged states against the Tenant contract, ticket #32 and the prototype's numbers. `verify.json` is the verdict (34/34 pass); `report.json` is the harness log with timestamps, gestures and the probe values.

What the numbered screenshots show: `01-book` the Sketchbook tab after its first click, the desktop created on show at 1:1 and centred on the 1920x1006 page with the window 620x500 at (980, 551) (the prototype's (740, 460) on its 1440x972 canvas), spread 1, no ink, previous disabled; `02-drawn` after a 8-motion drag of (64, 24) px across the left page — one stroke of 9 points, the ink on screen under the pencil cursor; `03-turning` two frames after a click on the next arrow — the paper turn in flight (progress 0.13, the face SubViewport live, mode 4); `04-turned` the turn landed after 515 ms on spread 2, blank, previous enabled; `05-turned-back` after the previous arrow (521 ms), spread 1 with its stroke back; `06-before-hidden` after a -100x-30 px drag on the title bar — the window at (880, 521), its page and ink moved with it; `07-hidden` the Map tab (no Tenant, plain white) after a drag across the hidden page and a click on the hidden next arrow — the Sketchbook page is frozen: `_process` ticks 133 -> 133 over 20 frames, `_input` count 27 -> 27, still one stroke on spread 1 and no turn, the finished-ink SubViewport at mode 1 and the paper-turn face at 0, the frame's total draw calls 40, the white-page baseline of 40 at launch (54 with the book shown); `08-resumed` the Sketchbook tab again, pixel-identical to `06`, ticks running again (138 -> 158); `09-resized` the window at the 1440x900 minimum, tenant 1440x844.5, the desktop shrunk to 0.869 with the window 539x434 at (650, 429) inside it and the stroke kept; `10-restored` back at 1920x1080, the desktop at 1:1 and the window where it was.
