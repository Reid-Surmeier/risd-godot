# video_player playtest evidence (build/v0.1.0, the Fly Through player as the Video Player Tenant)

Produced by `scripts/playtest.sh video_player docs/evidence/video-player` on Godot 4.7.2 (X display :99, OpenGL ES over llvmpipe, window 1920x1080 then 1440x900). The harness (`modules/video_player/playtest/harness.gd`, on `testing/harness_base.gd`) builds the Shell with the player in the Video Player Tab and nothing in the other Tabs, drives it with real mouse and key events through `Input.parse_input_event`, and reaches the player only through `ShellInterface.tenant_state`. Verified by `modules/video_player/playtest/verify.py`, which re-hashes the five preview videos against `media/manifest.json`, re-hashes every screenshot, re-reads pixels and checks the logged states against the Tenant contract and ticket #31. `verify.json` is the verdict (57/57 pass); `report.json` is the harness log with timestamps, gestures and the probe values.
