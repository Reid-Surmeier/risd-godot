VERDICT: Y

Y improves **warm and art at both embedded sizes**, meeting the two-view threshold without a clear still-image regression.

1. **Warm:** Y’s herringbone reads as individual wooden planks. X’s fine parallel grain creates denser striping, especially in the illuminated left foreground. Y preserves that warm pool and the transition into shadow.
2. **Art:** Y makes the foreground calmer and the broad parquet pattern easier to read at 720. At 1600, it retains enough directional grain to feel wooden while giving the paintings stronger visual priority.
3. **Entry:** A smaller improvement: Y’s pattern remains legible in the shallow visible floor area; X looks more finely streaked.
4. **Tradeoff:** Y loses some fine grain and looks slightly blockier at 1600. I do not see an obvious loss of useful material detail, excessive plank contrast, or flattened lighting. Its broader pattern hierarchy is closer to the references’ readable surface treatment.

**Motion remains unverified.** This verdict covers still images, including the held warm frames. Video files are present, but I did not inspect continuous playback; these images cannot establish whether Y introduces or reduces crawl, shimmer, or ripple.
