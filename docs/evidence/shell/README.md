# shell playtest evidence (wip/shell-bottom on build/v0.1.0)

Produced by `scripts/playtest.sh shell` on Godot 4.7.2 (X display, real mouse and key events through `Input.parse_input_event`, window 1920x1080 then 1440x900). The harness builds the Shell with its own registry — the dummy tenant in five Tabs, a grey Callable-built tenant in `phone` (so a page cross-fade shows in pixels), nothing in `playground` — so the game's main scene is free to register the real Tenants. Verified by `modules/shell/playtest/verify.py`, which re-hashes every screenshot, re-reads the films' pixels and checks the logged states, signals and tenant counters against the interface contract. `verify.json` is the verdict (48/48 pass); `report.json` is the harness log with timestamps.

What the numbered screenshots show (the bar now sits along the **bottom** of the window, the page fills the space above it): `01-launch` seven fixed tabs (Map, Sketchbook, 3D Viewer, Video Player, Collection, Playground, Phone — all seven with Muse-drawn titles, cut to "M…", "Sk…", … at seven-tab width) with Collection active after its launch grow and fade, page white; `02-map` / `03-sketchbook` the pages after a click on each tab (the dummy tenants are white, so only the tenant counters in `report.json` tell them apart: the hidden Map tenant's `_process` and input counts stand still once the fade has settled, the shown one's run); `04-fixed-kept` after a click where the Map tab's close button would be and a refused `close_tab` (`shell.tab_fixed`); `05-stub-blank` the stub's Blank Page as an eighth tab with its close button; `06-phone` after closing it, the Phone page (the grey Callable-built tenant, created on that first show); `07-collection` after a click on the Collection tab from Phone; `08-playground` the Playground page (no tenant registered here, plain white); `09-resized` the window at the 1440x900 minimum, bar re-fitted along the bottom; `10-restored` back at 1920x1080. `sheet-bar.png` is the toolbar band of 01-08.

The films (`frames/`, every frame with its time and the Shell's state in `report.json`): `film-launch.png` the launch — frame 0 has the Collection tab stub-sized with the stub's pressed tint in its own place (its neighbours and the stub still), it grows to its fitted width over the next ~0.5 s with its title fading in, and only then does its page show and its tenant exist (`switch_settled` at ~0.8 s after mount); `film-map-click.png` a click on Map — the clicked tab dips with the pressed tint in the first frame (75 ms) and is white again by 277 ms, the page cross-fade settling 213 ms after the click; `film-switch.png` a click on Collection from the grey Phone page — the dip on the Collection tab, then the page area blending from grey through lighter greys to white over ~200 ms (the verifier reads those pixels), `switch_settled` 226 ms after the click.

`browser/` is the Web export of build d59e62e (`scripts/export-web.sh`) driven in headless Chrome (software WebGL, 1920x1080) through the DevTools protocol by `modules/shell/playtest/browser_play.py`: real clicks on the tabs, the Collection page's controls and a card, the stub and the Blank Page's close button, with a few frames caught during the Map switch and the stub's grow. `browser/sheet-bar.png` is the toolbar band (the bottom of the window) across the run. The three-size run of the whole game is in `../v0.1.0/browser/`.
