# shell playtest evidence (wip/playground-phone on build/v0.1.0)

Produced by `scripts/playtest.sh shell` on Godot 4.7.2 (X display, real mouse and key events through `Input.parse_input_event`, window 1920x1080 then 1440x900). Six fixed Tabs since the owner folded the Phone Tab into the Playground desktop (2026-09-14, #62). The harness builds the Shell with its own registry — the dummy tenant in four Tabs, a grey Callable-built tenant in `playground` (so a page cross-fade shows in pixels), nothing in `video_player` — so the game's main scene is free to register the real Tenants. Verified by `modules/shell/playtest/verify.py`, which re-hashes every screenshot, re-reads the films' pixels and checks the logged states, signals and tenant counters against the interface contract. `verify.json` is the verdict (48/48 pass); `report.json` is the harness log with timestamps.

What the numbered screenshots show (the bar along the bottom of the window, the page above it): `01-launch` six fixed tabs (Map, Sketchbook, 3D Viewer, Video Player, Collection, Playground) with Collection active after its launch grow and fade, page white; `02-map` / `03-sketchbook` the pages after a click on each tab (the dummy tenants are white; the tenant counters in `report.json` tell them apart: the hidden Map tenant's counts stand still once the fade has settled, the shown one's run); `04-fixed-kept` after a click where the Map tab's close button would be and a refused `close_tab` (`shell.tab_fixed`); `05-stub-blank` the stub's Blank Page as a seventh tab with its close button; `06-grey` after closing it, the Playground page (the grey Callable-built tenant, created on that first show); `07-collection` after a click on the Collection tab from the grey page; `08-untenanted` the Video Player page (no tenant registered here, plain white); `09-resized` the window at the 1440x900 minimum, bar re-fitted; `10-restored` back at 1920x1080. `sheet-bar.png` is the toolbar band of 01-08.

The films (`frames/`, every frame with its time and the Shell's state in `report.json`; `film-*.png` lay them out with those values): `film-launch.png` the launch — the Collection tab stub-sized with the pressed tint, growing to its fitted width, and only then its page and tenant; `film-map-click.png` a click on Map — the dip, then the page cross-fade; `film-switch.png` a click on Collection from the grey page — the dip, and the page blending from grey through lighter greys to white over ~200 ms.

`browser/` predates the six-tab strip (seven tabs, a Phone tab): it is the Web export of build d59e62e (`scripts/export-web.sh`) driven in headless Chrome (software WebGL, 1920x1080) through the DevTools protocol by `modules/shell/playtest/browser_play.py`: real clicks on the tabs, the Collection page's controls and a card, the stub and the Blank Page's close button, with a few frames caught during the Map switch and the stub's grow. `browser/sheet-bar.png` is the toolbar band (the bottom of the window) across the run. The three-size run of the whole game is in `../v0.1.0/browser/`.
