## What matched, and what did not

- **T1** (`sheets/T1-virgin-and-child-15.108.jpg`): Seated Virgin with long hair and circlet, blue bodice under gold-brown mantle; Child on her right knee turned to an open book on her lap; stepped base. Front, both sides and back are filmed. Limit: Back is filmed only blurred at 105.70s (dark green, flat); the four saved official photos show front and three-quarter/side views, not the back.
- **T2** (`sheets/T2-god-save-the-queens-2020.55.jpg`): Bright metal domed lid with ring finial, cylindrical body with blue-and-white lattice, roundel of Christ crowned with thorns in a red robe, the words GOD SAVE THE QUEENS legible at 110.50s, blue Delft-style sailing boat panel at 105.70s, skirted base on four scroll feet. Limit: Official photos are marked in_copyright (artist 1994-1997): reference only, not a texture source without the owner clearing it. The west face of the body is not filmed.
- **T3** (`sheets/T3-monstrance-40.002.jpg`): Gilt tower monstrance: crocketed open spire with finial, glazed central chamber with flying buttresses and pinnacles, two knops on the stem, wide lobed star foot. Limit: Fine tracery and foot engraving are not resolved in the 1080p source.
- **T4** (`sheets/T4-communion-beaker-1992.051.jpg`): Plain flared silver beaker with everted lip, twisted rope collar above a stepped spreading foot; height consistent with 14 cm beside the 8.9 cm pyx. Two other on-view cups were compared and rejected by shape (Kiddush Cup 2016.17 is a stemmed goblet; Beaker 1991.033 is a dark straight tumbler). Limit: The engraved scrollwork and figure medallion on the body are not resolved in the source, so the match rests on form, the rope collar and being the only such beaker returned as on view.
- **T5** (`sheets/T5-pyx-30.011.jpg`): Small round box with conical lid, cross finial on a knob, hinge and clasp lugs at the rim; proportions match the 8.9 cm catalogue pyx. Limit: Enamel quatrefoils and blue ground are washed out to grey in the source; match rests on form, hardware and being the only pyx returned as on view.
- **T6** (`sheets/T6-christ-in-majesty-2014.110.jpg`): Narrow ivory relief figure about 2.5 times taller than wide: rounded head with nimbus outline, shoulders, horizontal bands at the chest, book-side projection at mid-height, two feet on a small base. Proportion matches 15.9 x 6.4 cm. Limit: Face and drapery detail are not resolved; match rests on silhouette, colour, proportion and being the only single-figure medieval ivory returned as on view. Back is unseen (object lies on its mount).
- **T7** (`sheets/T7-pax-52.002.jpg`): Pointed-arch shell plaque with white relief figures on a blue-grey ground inside a metal rim; the dark silver back with its handle is seen from the south-east at 107.30s. Limit: Relief subject is not resolved in the source beyond white figures on blue under an arch.
- **L1** (`sheets/L-low-case-prints-unidentified.jpg`): Very small upright work on paper, a few centimetres across: a pale central form on a darker ground with a light border, on a cream mount much larger than the image. Limit: Subject, technique and label text are unreadable at native resolution. No catalogue candidate could be compared.
- **L2** (`sheets/L-low-case-prints-unidentified.jpg`): Monochrome image slightly wider than tall, roughly three times the width of L1: a dark-toned scene with a seated or half-length figure with bowed head on the right and a second form on the left. Limit: Subject, technique and label text are unreadable at native resolution. No catalogue candidate could be compared.

## Limits

- **Low case identities are open.** The root review calls the two works manuscript leaves; at native resolution I can only confirm two monochrome images on cream mounts. A legible label photo or a closer frame is needed. Do not build them from a guessed catalogue record.
- **Unseen surfaces.** Not filmed: the underside of every object, the back of the ivory (T6), the west face of the ceramic (T2). The back of the Virgin (T1) is filmed only blurred. The tall case was filmed from all four sides, the low case from its north and south sides only.
- **Occlusion.** No slot in either case appears empty or hidden in any frame, but an object smaller than about 2 cm would not be resolved.
- **On-view flag.** The catalogue `onView` value says an object is on display somewhere in the museum, not in which case. Placement here rests on the photo comparison.
- **Positions and scale** are source-relative. Nothing here was measured.
- **Rights.** The T2 photographs are marked `in_copyright` by the museum; the other six are marked `public`. Use T2 photos as reference only until the owner decides.

## Method and checks

1. Read the existing catalogue caches first (4,134 cached records). They already held T1, T5, T6 and T7 as on-view candidates.
2. Then 28 free API requests (20 with `field_on_view=1`, 7 by id) and 9 catalogue pages with 27 photographs, through the Scrapling Fetcher the root already uses; plain `curl` gets a Cloudflare 403. Queries stopped when the coordinator asked.
3. Passed: all 151 rows returned by on-view queries have `onView` true; all 7 by-id records match the accession they were fetched for; three frame hashes equal the root's.
4. Not verified: the `field_on_view` parameter name comes from the root research note citing the official API documentation, which I did not re-read; only its behaviour was checked. Label text in both cases. Any metric position.
5. `git diff --check` passes. `scripts/check.sh` was not run: its Godot import step would write `.import` files beside pending files outside this folder, which this task may not touch. No source changed.

Rebuild: `fetch_api.py`, `fetch_photos.py`, `decode_frames.py`, `make_sheets.py`, `build_ledger.py` in this folder.
