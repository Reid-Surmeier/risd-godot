# Readable jump poses and arm clearance — 2026-10-01

Research for [issue 231](https://github.com/Reid-Surmeier/risd-godot/issues/231), after the owner reported a floating jump and idle arms intersecting the torso. **Author readable poses, select flight/landing from physics, and check the skinned surface rather than only joint angles.** No additional mesh generation, animation framework, or rig replacement is required to test these fixes.

## What the current prototype actually does

Audited baseline: commit `4ca9358b9ff60cebe3d78c1659d0bd5a89ab05bb`, [demo.gd](../../image-work/character-pilot/live-demo/demo.gd) and [shared pose bake](../../image-work/character-pilot/iterations/motion-diagnosis/correct.py). These observations describe that baseline, not subsequent fixes:

- The one-second jump has seven poses, generated mainly by rotating upper/lower legs and arms. The crouch key is at .08 seconds, takeoff is at .10, but entering the clip crossfades for .167 seconds. The transition lasts longer than the anticipation, diluting its visual weight.
- The visual root is translated to cancel the lowest sole's height on **every frame**, including flight. That raises/lowers the whole figure to cancel the animated sole offset; airborne knee lift cannot keep its own authored relation to the physical body's origin. This is a strong mechanism to test, not a measured claim that it explains every pixel of the owner's report.
- Physical launch uses velocity 3.6 and gravity 9.8; ideal same-level flight is `2*3.6/9.8≈.735` seconds. Actual collision is discrete. The landing-compression key is tied to .84 seconds rather than the actual collision, and the clip ends at one second even if terrain/contact timing changes.
- Selected `fitted-*` profiles use idle two-bone IK reach `.4` and lateral offset `.08`; `.35`/`.07` are only the shared bake's fallback defaults. The projected pole is `(side*.3,-1,0)`. The recent fix preserves wrist orientation relative to forearm; it does **not** establish a gap between the palm/forearm surface and torso. Tool rotations applied after the base clip can also replace the jump's arm pose.

## Pose design: a jump needs a readable change of shape

Animation Mentor's first-party tutorial by supervising animator Jason Martinsen blocks compression, powered extension, offset airborne legs, landing absorption and settle, then checks front/side views and hip arcs. It supports changing the figure's shape and timing, not just moving an unchanged pose vertically. Its athletic asymmetric/hero landing is not this character's style. Use a smaller, friendly hop with clear compression and recovery; retain rigid head geometry. [Original lesson](https://www.animationmentor.com/blog/tutorial-animate-a-jump-and-land/).

Suggested blocking for this pilot, **an authored adaptation rather than recovered GameCube jump data**:

| Stage | Readable pose and physical trigger |
| --- | --- |
| Anticipation | Grounded foot plants; pelvis/body descend between flexing knees, arms swing slightly back/out. Avoid hiding hands behind the broad torso. Entry blend must finish before the strongest crouch. |
| Takeoff | Legs/body extend and arms drive forward/up within head clearance. Apply the physical impulse on the same update as this extension; a crouch continuing after launch reads as levitation. |
| Ascent/apex | Release foot plants; knees tuck visibly while pelvis follows the physical arc. Maintain an outward elbow silhouette. Ease pose change near apex without freezing the physical body's height. |
| Descent/contact | Extend feet for landing when velocity turns downward. Actual `move_and_slide()` contact ends airborne pose selection; landing starts from the displayed pose rather than jumping to a fixed clip timestamp. |
| Compression/recovery | Both feet support a short pelvis drop, followed by a smaller settle. Then blend into the actual Idle/Walk/Run/Dash state. Impact audio fires once at contact; visual settle may continue afterwards. |

These are new pose targets to judge at the original game camera. None of the cited demos guarantees good animation for our broad head, short legs, tool holding, or original screenshot identity.

## Idle arms: positional clearance and wrist orientation are separate

The [Rigify control documentation](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.5/addons/rigging/rigify/rig_features.html#limbs) exposes IK/FK snapping, stretch control and pole selection; the [basic setup guide](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/addons/rigging/rigify/basics.html#rigify-human-alignment-tips) calls for a slight knee/elbow bend. These stabilize articulation. They do not imply collision avoidance.

For our existing analytic two-bone bake, first move the idle **wrist target** outside the actual torso silhouette while maintaining reach between `abs(l1-l2)` and `l1+l2` with a margin. Preserve the existing elbow pole when that one-variable probe clears the skin; adjust the pole only if the evaluated forearm still crosses the belly in other poses. Derive left/right targets in character space from the posed torso/palm extents, not camera space. Preserve hand-to-forearm orientation, then check sleeve, forearm, thumb and palm together. Neither the source rig nor an arbitrary numeric lateral offset guarantees clearance.

Use the evaluated skinned mesh for clearance checks. A coarse torso/head proxy can cheaply flag penetration, but validate that proxy against rendered geometry, and exclude the shoulder attachment region. Test both hands across idle, locomotion return, tool changes, jump poses and all blend intermediates. A passing joint-angle check or physics capsule does not prove that visible palms clear the belly. Correct shape weights only if safe skeletal targets still collapse the wrist seam; do not mask a pose bug by editing textures or hiding arms.

Godot 4.7.2 blends an omitted bone track against its initial/rest value only with `AnimationMixer.deterministic=true`. The default is `false`: weights are normalized over animations that contain the track; a channel omitted by every active animation retains its previous or scripted pose. This corrects the earlier unconditional rest-blending claim. Keep explicit animated channels and intentional tool-layer ownership. Transition modes distinguish immediate, synchronized and at-end playback, with separate crossfade/reset control. The existing manual player can implement the small phase sequence; add an AnimationTree only if graph editing helps this prototype. [Pinned 4.7.2 setting and default](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf001b61586d9930840f2f1394092c079/doc/classes/AnimationMixer.xml#L308-L315), [weight normalization](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf001b61586d9930840f2f1394092c079/scene/animation/animation_mixer.cpp#L1162-L1276), [zero-weight application guard](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf001b61586d9930840f2f1394092c079/scene/animation/animation_mixer.cpp#L1903-L1908), [transition definitions](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.3/classes/class_animationnodestatemachinetransition.html). The independent review's broader primary-source evidence is recorded in [Opus primary sources](character-opus-primary-sources-2026-10-01.md).

## Diagnostic trial evidence, not acceptance

The root agent's native skinned-surface lateral-ray reproduction found **105 distal arm vertices inside the body/upper-thigh surface**, maximum penetration `.1073575` target units, in the rejected idle. Changing only the selected idle wrist-target lateral offset from `.08` to `.22` produced zero flagged interior vertices with minimum measured gap `.02846`, without changing the elbow pole. The current [quality receipt](../../image-work/character-pilot/live-demo/evidence/quality-check.json) records that zero-interior result across 1,347 distal vertices. This supports the smaller target-only fix; it does not establish clearance for every possible animation or viewpoint.

The new [physical jump trial receipt](../../image-work/character-pilot/live-demo/evidence/jump-pose-check.json) records anticipation compression `.1035312`, landing compression `.1390322`, airborne ankle lift `.2243873`, and arm displacement `.4465800` target units. Its traced phases are Anticipate, Ascend, Descend, Land and Ground, with collision contact at update 52. The implementation trial removes airborne sole normalization and selects landing from contact. These demonstrate measurable pose changes and event alignment, **not accepted visual quality**. Normal-size video inspection and the independent review remain separate from these numerical receipts.

## Minimum evidence before claiming the fixes

1. Capture the actual game camera plus front/oblique views of neutral, deepest crouch, launch, apex, descent, contact, compression and recovery; inspect normal-speed loops as well as slow playback. Normal-scale silhouette changes must be visible without zooming into a bone overlay.
2. Record phase, vertical velocity, floor contact, clip position, pelvis height, knee flex and root correction. Airborne root correction must not force lifted soles onto the physical origin. Contact must select landing once on flat floor and a different-height platform, without an early fixed-timestamp impact.
3. Sample both sides' forearm/palm/torso clearance through all poses and actual runtime blends; preserve the existing wrist-relative-rest regression check. Head/hand and held-tool clearance deserve the same visual review during arm drive.
4. Keep gait speed/phase, rigid-head shape, materials, blink transitions, contact audio/dust suppression, no air retrigger, desktop and touch controls in regression coverage. Physics tests establish correct flight, not readable posing; both checks are necessary.

## Independent-review implementation follow-up

Round 2 exposed a standing-jump steering case that kept world ankle anchors while the actor accelerated, a whole-model 20-degree sprint lean snap, and net/head intersection that a single idle sample missed. The fixes choose the moving landing from current speed/input, subtract compression already carried by the displayed-pose blend, smooth whole-model lean, preserve the tool carry arm and check real prop surfaces through every jump tick. The added regression cases cover direction pressed with the jump, at the apex and at contact, and a second jump pressed during recovery.

Godot documents `BoneAttachment3D.on_skeleton_update()` as the operation that refreshes the attachment position when the skeleton updates. The manually advanced prototype explicitly invokes it after its procedural pose, ensuring headless clearance tests sample the same current prop placement as the rendered figure. [Godot BoneAttachment3D](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_boneattachment3d.html#class-boneattachment3d-method-on-skeleton-update).

The transition starts from a constant animation containing the displayed skeletal pose, then uses the existing native AnimationPlayer blend. This preserves an interrupted transition without making contact IK feed back into the next base pose. [Godot AnimationPlayer](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_animationplayer.html).

Local evidence for candidate `3d9fb0309b55`: repository baseline passed; native pose, appearance and browser checks passed; 632 tool poses / 1,634 surface queries returned no interior samples and a minimum actual head-surface gap of 0.04338 units. Both mitten close-ups show the isolated hand sampler's seam repair. Round 4 rejected the deferred-contact regression described below; the corrected c0aec056189b candidate received independent round-5 prototype approval. Source fidelity, faceted mitten topology and exact original audio remain separately open.

Round 3 found the axe off-hand snapping back because its old overlay blend timer had already saturated during flight. Regrip now owns a half-second timer, capped Cartesian target motion and a blended elbow pole. All 48 phase/steering cases (1,920 regrip poses) pass the actual arm/body interior check and stay below 0.06 units per tick; the measured maximum is 0.04315. Land clip changes carry their existing compression, and descending physical motion is capped to forecast floor contact to avoid a root overshoot and visible pre-contact lift.

The owner requested less idle rocking. This is a deliberate departure from the raw WAIT1 amplitude: native position keys interpolate halfway towards the first pose and rotation keys slerp halfway towards it, preserving period and looping. A deep copy of the imported AnimationLibrary isolates these edits. Actual FK comparison measures half the hip motion and approximately 52% of the head motion; the new side-by-side proof shows the untouched imported idle and adjusted idle using the same mesh, materials and camera.

A subsequent contact probe found that limiting the body to exactly the floor plane can leave `is_on_floor()` false for one more tick. The reduced solver velocity then rewinds the velocity-selected flight pose and weakens the next tick’s impact cue. The smallest tested correction requests motion one existing `safe_margin` past the plane and lets `move_and_slide()` resolve contact. Godot documents this property as its extra collision-recovery margin (default 0.001), affecting consistent floor detection. This is a local test-supported application of that contract, not a documented guarantee that a centre ray alone represents every terrain shape. [Godot CharacterBody3D safe_margin](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_characterbody3d.html#class-characterbody3d-property-safe-margin). The added checks require monotonic descent pose, preserved flat-hop impact and bounded hand movement at contact; the rejected exact-plane version fails the descent check.

Expanding the contact test to 200 direction/start/timing/gait cases exposed 30 failures in the margin-only correction. Requesting native `apply_floor_snap()` only after the sweep predicts this tick’s touchdown resolves contact through Godot’s collider query instead; all 200 cases then record zero deferred contacts/flight rewinds and preserve the original impact. This is the selected follow-up correction. Godot documents this method as applying floor snap independent of velocity and doing nothing when already on the floor. [Godot apply_floor_snap](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_characterbody3d.html#class-characterbody3d-method-apply-floor-snap).
