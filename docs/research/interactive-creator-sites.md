# Interactive creator sites adjacent to KiiiKiii’s “Delulu World”

Checked 2026-09-22. This shortlist follows the credited creator network around Rachel Yeeun Kim (`@9imyen`), BYHEYONE, Junejuunny, and OKOK Services. Instagram credits were read from the creators’ public profiles with Scrape Creators (five profile requests; five credits). Project ownership and descriptions were cross-checked against direct creator posts or [OKOK Services’ first-party portfolio](https://okok.services/). “Live” means the project URL returned a page when checked; it does not guarantee that every interaction still works.

## What the creator trail shows

- **Rachel (`@9imyen`) is the most useful source for art-directed campaign sites.** Her current public posts directly identify KiiiKiii and Wine, Women & Song and name the developers, which makes each collaborator another productive trail.
- **OKOK Services is the best source for more working examples.** The studio developed both KiiiKiii sites and maintains a first-party portfolio linking Balming Tiger, SHINee, ARE YOU OKOK?, Jiwoo Moon, Local Remote, Shannon Lim, and other interaction-heavy work.
- **BYHEYONE is the concept-level bridge.** Its public feed explicitly claims creative direction/planning for WhyKiiiKiii and credits the broader visual team, including Rachel.
- **Junejuunny is adjacent creative direction, not a separate web-design archive.** Her [404 Everywhere post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DUyOE2vknnb/) says she contributed to creative direction, while OKOK’s [Delulu site post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DU-gwZaEnzX/) places her on the site’s creative team. I found no separately credited website in her current public grid, so attributing one to her would overstate the evidence.
