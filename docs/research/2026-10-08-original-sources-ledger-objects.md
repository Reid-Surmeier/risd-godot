## Zoom against download: what the catalogue really serves

The earlier check (#180) only saw the 1324 px zoom image. That is not the maximum. Each carousel entry carries three addresses, and the third is a dialog, not a file:

1. `data-preview-url`: 600 px wide.
2. `data-zoom-url`: 1324 px wide when the master is at least that large (112 of 115 measured zoom renditions are 1324 px wide (the others: 1300, 512); 9 zoom images measured over the network tonight, the rest are saved copies in the repo).
3. `data-download-url` (`/object/<node>/download/<asset>`): answers with a dialog that links three files on `risdmuseum.cdn.picturepark.com/d/<id>/`: Screen-resolution JPEG, High-resolution JPEG, High-resolution TIFF. On the 198 object pages here, 1040 of the 1040 photographs marked `public` carry it and 0 of the 29 others (in_copyright or unmarked) do.
4. 772 of those 1069 photographs also sit in the museum's Micrio viewer (`<micr-io id>` in the carousel entry). `https://iiif.micr.io/<id>/info.json` gives the full size; the IIIF service returns up to 17.55 megapixels per request and the whole image through 1024 px tiles.
