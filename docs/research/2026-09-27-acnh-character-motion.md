# New Horizons PlayerBody motion: source and retarget route

Research for [Research authentic character motion and a compatible retarget route](https://github.com/Reid-Surmeier/risd-godot/issues/151), checked 2026-09-27. **Decision:** inspect motion from the game's own `RomFS/CharactorAnimation.Nin_NX_NVN.zs` against the chosen `PlayerBody` skeleton first. If a required clip cannot be recovered and transferred, trial the already measured KayKit Rogue motion as a **non-authentic retarget fallback**. Neither route has yet been proven on a `PlayerBody` payload in Godot; this report authorizes no runtime asset or authenticity claim.

## Evidence and limits

- The [uploader of the selected New Horizons 2.0.0 DAE/PNG export](https://www.reddit.com/r/ac_newhorizons/comments/qtmv3x/all_models_textures_ripped_for_acnh_200_daepng/) says the models are rigged but animations were omitted. Our [prior bounded archive-header inspection](2026-09-27-character-asset-sources.md) located `Model/PlayerBody.Nin_NX_NVN/PlayerBody.dae` without reading its model payload. The DAE archive therefore supplies a **candidate target rig, not motion**. No joint hierarchy, skin weights, or clip channels from that file have been inspected.
- A [first-hand ACNH animation-mod author](https://gamebanana.com/tuts/19762) identifies `CharactorAnimation.Nin_NX_NVN.zs` in RomFS as the animation container, reports that its motion applies to villagers **and the player**, and describes previewing it with a model in Switch-Toolbox and exporting `.bfska` or `.smd`. This is a credible source-compatible path, not our own inspection of the archive. The author observed unscaled motion and stretched legs when previewed directly; their bone-renaming workaround is for a game mod and is **not** a validated Godot conversion recipe. The tutorial reports 979 animations in its worked export, but does not certify the names, contents, or usability of the required player clips for our version.
- [Switch-Toolbox's maintainer](https://github.com/KillzXGaming/Switch-Toolbox/blob/master/README.md) documents BFRES skeletal preview and animation subsection import/export. The tool is archived; its latest version does not open XCI/NSP/NCA directly. The path starts from an already available, lawfully obtained **extracted RomFS**, not a game image passed to Toolbox. Its capabilities do not prove a `.bfska` to glTF conversion or reliable transfer to the separate COLLADA export.

| Required behavior | New Horizons evidence today | Candidate if no suitable native clip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Idle, walk, run if available | Shared animation archive is identified; exact clip names, duration, loop flags and player playback are **unverified**. | Rogue `Idle` and `Walking_A` are inspected; no Rogue run verified. Measure a faster walk before representing it as a run. |
| Start, stop, turns | No exact native clips verified. Determine whether transitions are separate clips or the game blends locomotion. | Controller transitions and rotation using the measured in-place walk; label these **constructed**, not Nintendo motion. |
| Look and artwork interaction | No exact native clips verified. A game animation may be unrelated to looking at art. | Rogue `Interact` is inspected; head aim and artwork trigger would be project-authored. Do not relabel the gesture as authentic. |

The fallback has a concrete measurement base: the pinned [KayKit Rogue source GLB](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0/672074b73ba276876a19e8816ecdc5241817ab47/addons/kaykit_character_pack_adventures/Characters/gltf/Rogue.glb) and [creator license](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0/blob/672074b73ba276876a19e8816ecdc5241817ab47/LICENSE.txt) were inspected in the earlier [grounded-character study](gallery-grounded-3d-character-plan.md). That study recorded 41 joints, 76 animation entries, 1.066667-second `Idle` and `Walking_A`, 1.3-second `Interact`, and an in-place root for those clips. `Walking_A`'s left toe traveled about 0.513 source units fore/aft and 0.008–0.157 vertically. The existing [gallery rig derivative](../../modules/shell/prototype/gallery_walk4/rig/SOURCE.md) already uses only `Idle`, `Walking_A`, and `Interact`. **Those measurements establish the donor, not a successful retarget to PlayerBody.** KayKit's CC0 license covers its own source; it does not change the status of Nintendo's mesh or animation data.

## Small, falsifiable transfer trial

1. On an authorized local copy of the selected game version, inventory the extracted `CharactorAnimation.Nin_NX_NVN.zs` and one original-format PlayerBody model. Record archive hashes and a shortlist of clips with their exact names, frame counts, loop flags, skeleton track names, root translation, and visible behavior. Inspect **idle and walk first**; add run, start/stop, turns, look, and interaction only when actual clips support those labels. Do not bulk export all 979 merely to locate two motions. Keep the untouched source files outside the shipped runtime.
2. Preview one native idle and one walk on the original player skeleton in Toolbox. Compare its joint names, hierarchy, rest pose, axis, and scale with the extracted DAE's skin controller. Export only those clips to an interchange path; Toolbox lists `.smd` export in the [first-hand workflow](https://gamebanana.com/tuts/19762), and [Blender Source Tools](https://github.com/Artfunkel/BlenderSourceTools) supplies the SMD bridge. Bake on a **copy** of the target rig in Blender, inspect feet and clothing, and export one GLB. If SMD loses required channels or the rigs differ, stop and record the failure; do not infer compatibility from a successful file import.
3. Import the target GLB as a scene into this project's Godot **4.7.2 Compatibility** runtime. For a separate source animation, import it as an `AnimationLibrary`; map source and target with `BoneMap` and `SkeletonProfileHumanoid` where the anatomy permits, manually verify every important joint, and inspect rest-pose effects. [Godot 4.7's retarget guide](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/assets_pipeline/retargeting_3d_skeletons.html) states that matching bone names alone is insufficient, position tracks can change body shape, position normalization uses hip height, and rest-axis overwrite can damage an asset. Use the same checks for the KayKit fallback. Prefer a preserved, inspected target rest pose over a forced humanoid mapping if its unusual proportions break the result. Godot recommends [glTF 2.0](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/assets_pipeline/importing_3d_scenes/available_formats.html) for 3D interchange.
4. Prove playback with the imported `AnimationPlayer`/`AnimationTree` on the assembled PlayerBody and clothing: idle loop, straight/diagonal walk, start/release at several gait phases, 90°/180° turns, looking while idle, artwork interaction, and cancellation by movement. [Godot's AnimationTree guide](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/animation/animation_tree.html) supports transitions, blend spaces, track filters, and one-shots; their availability is not proof that these particular clips blend well. Capture front/side/back continuous video in native Compatibility and the Web export. Log clip duration, scale, facing, root motion, planted-sole drift and floor penetration against the **actual** controller displacement, plus whether attached clothing follows the body. Compare motion at gameplay camera size. A separate earlier study proposed initial sole limits of 0.02 world units horizontal drift and 0.01 floor penetration at 1.75-unit character height; calibrate them to this model before using them as a gate.

**Authenticity label for any later build:** "New Horizons source animation" only for an inspected game clip demonstrably playing correctly on PlayerBody; "KayKit motion retargeted to New Horizons geometry" for the fallback; "project-authored transition/look/interaction" for controller blending or newly posed actions. A motion assembled from genuine clips can be described as mixed, with each component named. Game footage alone is a timing reference, not an extracted animation track. No checked source grants redistribution rights for Nintendo's extracted model or clips; resolve that separately before shipping either.

## What this resolves

The identified primary motion container and the bounded donor route answer **where to look** and **how to test Godot compatibility**. The exact required native clip inventory, transfer quality, and asset rights remain unknown until the small payload trial. No game archive, model payload, or new motion file was downloaded here; no paid call or runtime edit was made. Research spend: **$0**.
