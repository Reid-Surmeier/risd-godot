# Original 3D visitor identity: prototype result

For [#130](https://github.com/Reid-Surmeier/risd-godot/issues/130) under the [#116 gallery map](https://github.com/Reid-Surmeier/risd-godot/issues/116), 2026-09-26. Research and local prototype spend: **$0**. This is a visual and rig feasibility study, not an integrated or accepted visitor.

**The shortest viable path is a new original low-poly skin on the already-proven KayKit Rogue skeleton.** The [creator's pinned license](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0/blob/672074b73ba276876a19e8816ecdc5241817ab47/LICENSE.txt) explicitly places the pack under CC0. The [exact source GLB](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0/blob/672074b73ba276876a19e8816ecdc5241817ab47/addons/kaykit_character_pack_adventures/Characters/gltf/Rogue.glb) is preserved locally as [`Rogue.source.glb`](../../modules/shell/prototype/gallery_walk4/identity/Rogue.source.glb), SHA-256 `e825437cd4d2ee9c1960b517a74a69101e33eb409ae7fa8cedc7134a998fbb7d`, beside its unchanged [`KayKit-LICENSE.txt`](../../modules/shell/prototype/gallery_walk4/identity/KayKit-LICENSE.txt). No Nintendo geometry, game data, or textures are used.

The [Blender recipe](../../modules/shell/prototype/gallery_walk4/identity/build.py) removes every Rogue mesh and authors a round head, red soft cap, face, cream shirt with flush oxblood material bands, navy shorts, skin, and brown shoes. It binds 29 authored pieces to the original bones and joins them into one skinned mesh. The [exported GLB](../../modules/shell/prototype/gallery_walk4/identity/visitor_identity.glb) has 5,104 triangles, 10 lit material primitives, **no image textures**, one 41-joint skin, and all 76 imported animation entries. Blender CPU [idle](../../modules/shell/prototype/gallery_walk4/identity/visitor_identity_preview.png) and [walk](../../modules/shell/prototype/gallery_walk4/identity/visitor_identity_walk_preview.png) previews show an actual volume and skeletal pose change. Godot 4.7.2 headless import found `Skeleton3D` with 41 bones and `AnimationPlayer` with 76 entries, including `Idle`, `Walking_A/B/C`, and `Interact`. This verifies reuse of the animation rig, not sole contact quality.

The old [Muse turnaround](../../image-work/gallery-character/review/identity.webp) is a useful visual anchor: muted red cap, rounded head, dark hair, cream upper body, tiny legs and shoes. Its local review records `humanReviewed: false`, `accepted: false`, and a head-plus-cap height near 58% of its figure. The new geometry follows that direction and the owner's later **striped-shirt** instruction; it is a fresh design rather than a literal conversion of that 2D sheet. The Rogue itself remains a valid motion and lighting control, but its fantasy hair, tunic, belt and boots do not meet this identity.

Other researched assets did not shorten the path. [Tibo's Godot Plush](https://github.com/gtibo/Godot-Plush-Character) is an animated rig under [MIT](https://github.com/gtibo/Godot-Plush-Character/blob/main/license.md), but replacing its robot identity would also require a full skin. [Quaternius Universal Base Characters](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html) lists CC0 on the pack page while its [current license page](https://github.com/Quaternius/quaternius.github.io/blob/main/license.html) describes QAL v1.0; that ambiguity is avoidable here. The [Nintendo-era joint system](https://github.com/ACreTeam/ac-decomp/blob/09ca8e8b5b24e6ab44047ee980cf0088ad7ecb4c/include/c_keyframe.h) provides behavioral reference, not a redistributable asset library.

## What remains unachieved

1. **Visual acceptance.** The cap still reads broad and rounded; face, head ratio, shirt, and shoes need matched camera comparisons with the supplied house reference, the Muse sheet, and Rogue. CPU previews alone are insufficient. A front/rear/profile turntable and a gallery native/browser shot must be inspected at the intended camera.
2. **Grounding and light.** The skin uses lit materials but has not yet received the gallery's probe field. The current room bake has probes disabled, so the separate #139 bake/controller work is required. [Godot LightmapProbe](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_lightmapprobe.html) documents probes for moving objects; see the [grounded-character plan](gallery-grounded-3d-character-plan.md) for its Compatibility limits. No claim of room-lit behavior or cast shadows follows from the CPU preview.
3. **Motion quality.** Rigid one-bone weights can create visible shoulder/elbow joins, and Rogue's in-place gait may slide under the gallery controller. Walking_A is present and deforms the mesh; planted soles, turning, idle transition, gesture and rear/profile views have not passed visual review. A small weight pass should be judged against footage before replacing the rig source or animation clips.
4. **Integration and size.** This branch changes only prototype files and research. The shell still runs the Sprite3D visitor. Replacing its adapter and frozen `visitor_check.gd` acceptance requires a new implementation Issue naming that seam and preserving the #139 lighting/motion checks. The derivative GLB is 3.81 MB plus the pinned 3.62 MB source; production packaging can keep provenance in the repository while exporting only the derivative.

Native gallery evidence: pending a coordinated render slot after the rig and display workers' GPU passes. Source/prototype/check files are all under `modules/shell/prototype/gallery_walk4/identity/`; no files from their worktrees were edited.
