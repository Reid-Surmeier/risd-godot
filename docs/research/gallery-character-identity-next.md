# Original 3D visitor identity: prototype result

For [#130](https://github.com/Reid-Surmeier/risd-godot/issues/130) under the [#116 gallery map](https://github.com/Reid-Surmeier/risd-godot/issues/116), with integration acceptance in [#142](https://github.com/Reid-Surmeier/risd-godot/issues/142), 2026-09-26. Research and local prototype spend: **$0**. This is a visual and rig feasibility study, not an accepted visitor.

**The shortest viable path is a new original low-poly skin on the already-proven KayKit Rogue skeleton.** The [creator's pinned license](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0/blob/672074b73ba276876a19e8816ecdc5241817ab47/LICENSE.txt) explicitly places the pack under CC0. The [exact source GLB](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0/blob/672074b73ba276876a19e8816ecdc5241817ab47/addons/kaykit_character_pack_adventures/Characters/gltf/Rogue.glb) is preserved locally as [`Rogue.source.glb`](../../modules/shell/prototype/gallery_walk4/identity/Rogue.source.glb), SHA-256 `e825437cd4d2ee9c1960b517a74a69101e33eb409ae7fa8cedc7134a998fbb7d`, beside its unchanged [`KayKit-LICENSE.txt`](../../modules/shell/prototype/gallery_walk4/identity/KayKit-LICENSE.txt). No Nintendo geometry, game data, or textures are used.

The [Blender recipe](../../modules/shell/prototype/gallery_walk4/identity/build.py) removes every Rogue mesh and authors a round head, red soft cap, face, cream shirt with flush oxblood material bands, navy shorts, skin, and brown shoes. It binds 29 authored pieces to the original bones and joins them into one skinned mesh. The [exported GLB](../../modules/shell/prototype/gallery_walk4/identity/visitor_identity.glb) has 5,104 triangles, 10 lit material primitives, **no image textures**, one 41-joint skin, and all 76 imported animation entries. Blender CPU [idle](../../modules/shell/prototype/gallery_walk4/identity/visitor_identity_preview.png) and [walk](../../modules/shell/prototype/gallery_walk4/identity/visitor_identity_walk_preview.png) previews show an actual volume and skeletal pose change. Godot 4.7.2 headless import found `Skeleton3D` with 41 bones and `AnimationPlayer` with 76 entries, including `Idle`, `Walking_A/B/C`, and `Interact`. This verifies reuse of the animation rig, not sole contact quality.

The old [Muse turnaround](../../image-work/gallery-character/review/identity.webp) is a useful visual anchor: muted red cap, rounded head, dark hair, cream upper body, tiny legs and shoes. Its local review records `humanReviewed: false`, `accepted: false`, and a head-plus-cap height near 58% of its figure. The new geometry follows that direction and the owner's later **striped-shirt** instruction; it is a fresh design rather than a literal conversion of that 2D sheet. Matched native Godot Compatibility renders of [this identity](../../modules/shell/prototype/gallery_walk4/identity/visitor_identity_godot.png) and the [Rogue control](../../modules/shell/prototype/gallery_walk4/identity/rogue_control_godot.png) show that the new skin reads as a different visitor under the same camera and light. The [native render check](../../modules/shell/prototype/gallery_walk4/identity/native_render_check.gd) recreates that pair. Rogue remains a motion/lighting control, but its fantasy hair, tunic, belt and boots do not meet this identity. Those renders used Mesa llvmpipe, not the actual gallery lightmap or browser export.

Other researched assets did not shorten the path. [Tibo's Godot Plush](https://github.com/gtibo/Godot-Plush-Character) is an animated rig under [MIT](https://github.com/gtibo/Godot-Plush-Character/blob/main/license.md), but replacing its robot identity would also require a full skin. [Quaternius Universal Base Characters](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html) lists CC0 on the pack page while its [current license page](https://github.com/Quaternius/quaternius.github.io/blob/main/license.html) describes QAL v1.0; that ambiguity is avoidable here. The [Nintendo-era joint system](https://github.com/ACreTeam/ac-decomp/blob/09ca8e8b5b24e6ab44047ee980cf0088ad7ecb4c/include/c_keyframe.h) provides behavioral reference, not a redistributable asset library.

## What remains unachieved

1. **Visual acceptance.** The cap still reads broad and rounded; face, head ratio, shirt, and shoes need matched camera comparisons with the supplied house reference and Muse sheet. The standalone Godot/Rogue comparison is useful but insufficient. A front/rear/profile turntable and a gallery native/browser shot must be inspected at the intended camera.
2. **Grounding and light.** The rebased prototype can select this skin on the #139 grounded adapter. Its authored shoe sole lies at -0.015 source units, so the adapter anchors the actual sole rather than the bone origin. The inherited 480-frame rig check passed with 22 contacts, 0.0000004-unit maximum stance drift, and 0.000061-unit maximum shoe penetration (including 24 stop phases and a stationary pivot). This is an automated geometry check; changing room light and movement still need visual review. [Godot LightmapProbe](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_lightmapprobe.html) documents the moving-object capture; see the [grounded-character plan](gallery-grounded-3d-character-plan.md) for its Compatibility limits.
3. **Motion quality.** Rigid one-bone weights can create visible shoulder/elbow joins. Walking_A deforms the mesh and the inherited contact check passes, but turn, idle transition, gesture and rear/profile views have not passed visual review. A small weight pass should be judged against footage before replacing the rig source or animation clips.
4. **Integration and size.** The prototype now selects the identity with `?character=identity` on Web or `GALLERY_CHARACTER=identity` natively; the Rogue default remains the matched control. The derivative GLB is 3.81 MB plus the pinned 3.62 MB source; production packaging can keep provenance in the repository while exporting only the derivative. The identity retains its 10 surface colors without the Rogue's single-material lighting gain, so probe capture must be checked in the actual gallery.

Native gallery and browser evidence: pending the coordinated render window. All edits are in this isolated worktree; no files from the rig or display worktrees were edited.
