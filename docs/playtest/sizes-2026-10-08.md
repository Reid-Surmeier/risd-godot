or width more than 3 % or 2 cm (whichever is larger) from the catalogue figure. Catalogue strings
are read as height x width x depth; a framed or matted work is held by the picture inside, anything
else by its outer size without its label card.

What the numbers say, before any reading of footage:

- **All 23 Hall paintings and every framed painting in the rooms but one have a canvas at the
  catalogue size** (worst 1.2 %, the Madonna 58.196). That includes the Cézanne 43.255: canvas
  0.737 x 0.610 m built, 61 x 73.7 cm catalogued. The catalogue gives no frame sizes, so this
  check cannot say whether a frame's moulding is the right width; the 1 October audit

- The faults are almost all objects, not paintings: flat photograph stand-ins in the European
  gallery, the two tureens, and works shown leaning or lying that the script cannot tell from a
  wrong size.
- The Hall is measured on the nodes `walk4.gd` builds; a visitor sees their saved bake.
