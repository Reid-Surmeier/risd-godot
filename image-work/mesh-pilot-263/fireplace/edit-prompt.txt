Reference 1 is the official RISD Museum photograph of the Fireplace Surround by Hugnet Frères, Paris, 1900, accession 83.152: a tall Art Nouveau fireplace surround in carved walnut with turquoise ceramic tiles and a copper firebox. It is the sole design authority. Show this exact object alone and complete, from the carved flower at the very top to its two feet at the bottom, seen from the same viewpoint, in the same proportions and at the same size as in the reference, isolated on a plain seamless pure white background. Keep every carving exactly where it is: the three sunflower blooms and the scrolls on the crest, the engraved sunburst and the carved landscape relief on the upper panel, the two carved women leaning on the arch, their flowing hair and the stems running down both sides, the turquoise sunflower tiles, the copper firebox with its shelves and brass rim. Keep the photograph's own colours, wood grain and soft frontal light. Remove the wall, the baseboard, the marble floor, the bracket at the top left corner and every cast shadow. Do not add anything, do not restyle, do not simplify the carving, do not rotate the object. No text.
