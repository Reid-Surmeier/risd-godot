Reference 1 is a grey clay maquette of a sculpture. Reference 2 is the museum's photograph of the real sculpture. Repaint reference 1 exactly as it is drawn, with the same outline, the same viewpoint, the same size and place in the frame and every carved line where it is, as a flat colour map of the real material of reference 2: glazed earthenware in the colours of the photograph: the horse a warm chestnut brown with a paler mane and tail; the rider in a cream coat, green breeches, a black hat and black boots, with a pale face; the mound green with ochre; the stepped base pale cream with a green band along its top edge, with its own colour only. Keep its real markings: paint traces, stains, chips. Light it perfectly evenly from every side, as in a light tent, so that there is no shading at all: no shadows, no dark creases, no darkening in grooves, hair or hollows, no highlights. Matte. Plain seamless white background, no cast shadow, no plinth, no text.
