Repaint reference 1 exactly as it is drawn, with the same outline, the same viewpoint, the same size and place in the frame and every carved line where it is, as a flat colour map of the real material of reference 2: glazed earthenware: a cream-white horse, the rider in coloured glazes, a green and brown dragon and base, with its own colour only. Keep its real markings: paint traces, stains, chips. Light it perfectly evenly from every side, as in a light tent, so that there is no shading at all: no shadows, no dark creases, no darkening in grooves, hair or hollows, no highlights. Matte.
