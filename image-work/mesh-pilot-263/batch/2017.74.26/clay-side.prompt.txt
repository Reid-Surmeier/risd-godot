Reference 1 is the front of a sculpture as a clay maquette; the other references are photographs of the real sculpture from other sides. Draw this exact sculpture, in the same light grey matte clay and light, seen exactly from its own left side in true profile, camera level and at ninety degrees to the front: a small glazed earthenware model of a parrot perched upright on a tree stump, seen in profile: the body leans forward and the head bows, the hooked beak open; a thin vine stem runs from the beak down in front of the breast to the stump, standing clear of the body as an open loop, with vine leaves and a small bunch of grapes lying on the shoulder; the folded wings lie along the back and one long tail slants down behind the stump; the stump has cut knots and spreads at the foot, and is part of the piece and stays. Keep every proportion of the references. Show the whole object, centred, filling about three quarters of the frame. Plain seamless white background with no horizon line. Level camera. No cast shadow, no reflections, no text.
