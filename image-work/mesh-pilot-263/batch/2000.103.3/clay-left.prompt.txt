Reference 1 is the front of a white Regency dress on a headless dressmaker's form, as a clay maquette. Draw this exact dress on its form, in the same light grey matte clay and light, in pure side profile: the camera is level and at exactly ninety degrees to the front, looking at the dress's own left side. The front of the dress faces the left edge of the picture. From the side the form is a narrow upright shape: the bust and the folded collar toward the left, the high waist seam running level round the body just under the bust, the skirt falling straight with a little more fullness gathered at the back, the two bands of flounces running level round the hem. The near arm hangs at the side in its long sleeve, puffed at the shoulder, and hides part of the bodice; the far arm is hidden behind it. Do not draw a three-quarter view. Keep every height of reference 1, and the same short block stand. Show the whole object, centred, filling about three quarters of the frame height. Plain seamless white background with no horizon line. No cast shadow, no reflections, no text.
