Reference 1 is the front of a wooden sculpture. References 2 and 3 are video frames of the same sculpture hanging on a wall, seen at an angle. Draw this exact sculpture as an unpainted, matte, light grey clay maquette, seen exactly from its own left side in true profile, camera level and at ninety degrees to the front, so that the face points to the left of the picture. It is a tall oak figure of the crucified Christ without its cross: the head upright with a low turban-like crown, long hair and a short beard; both arms stretched straight out sideways, slightly raised, with open hands; a narrow flat chest with a long vertical split in the wood and carved ribs; a knee-length loincloth knotted at the hips with a ragged scalloped hem; thin straight legs together, the feet side by side pointing down. In this profile the carving is shallow, about one eighth as deep as it is tall; the back is nearly flat where it hangs against the wall; the near arm points straight at the viewer and is strongly foreshortened; the head tips slightly forward; the loincloth and knees stand a little proud of the chest. Keep every proportion of the references. Show the whole object, centred, filling about three quarters of the frame. Plain seamless white background with no horizon line. One even light grey clay tone. Level camera. No cast shadow, no wall, no reflections, no text.
