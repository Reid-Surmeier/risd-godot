Draw this exact sculpture, in the same light grey matte clay and light, seen exactly from its own left side in true profile, camera level and at ninety degrees to the front: a small glazed earthenware model of a parrot perched on a tall mound, seen in profile: the body arches forward and the large round head bows down, the hooked beak pointing at the ground; one long straight tail slants down behind the mound to half its height; two thin legs with clawed feet grip the top of the mound; the plumage is modelled as small raised dabs all over the head and body; the mound is fluted from top to bottom like blades of grass, widens to a flat foot, is part of the piece and stays. Keep every proportion of the references. Show the whole object, centred, filling about three quarters of the frame. Plain seamless white background with no horizon line. Level camera. No cast shadow, no reflections, no text.
