Reference 1 is the front of a sculpture as a clay maquette; the other references are photographs of the real sculpture from other sides. Draw this exact sculpture, in the same light grey matte clay and light, seen exactly from its own left side in true profile, camera level and at ninety degrees to the front: a small glazed earthenware figure of a standing man playing a horn: he holds a curved hunting horn to his mouth with his right hand, the bell of the horn resting against his right shoulder, and sets his left hand on his hip with the elbow out; he wears a wig with side curls, a knee-length coat with buttons, breeches and buckled shoes; a tree-stump support rises behind his legs to the hem of the coat; he stands on a low round domed base, which is part of the piece and stays. Keep every proportion of the references. Show the whole object, centred, filling about three quarters of the frame. Plain seamless white background with no horizon line. Level camera. No cast shadow, no reflections, no text.
