# sketchbook

## What callers get

`SketchbookInterface.create(deps)` returns a full-rect Control that satisfies the Shell's Tenant contract (`modules/shell/interface.gd`, ticket #24): it lays out from its own `size` / `resized`, never the root viewport, and `state()` is the harness probe. The Shell's demo registry maps `sketchbook` to it (map #23; ticket #32: two Tenants sharing nothing but the Shell, the book alone on its page, the Mixbox paintbox left out of v0.1.0 — no Mixbox file is in this repository, and the book needs no palette: it draws in tldraw's one ink). Every pixel file the window loads is checked before anything is built; a missing one returns `sketchbook.asset_missing` with the path.

The Page is the prototype's desktop with one window (owner correction on map #23: a Tab shows the prototype's desktop): the 1440x972 white canvas kept 1:1 and centred where it fits, shrunk to fit a smaller Page, with the sketchbook window at (740, 460), 620x500, where the prototype's `desktop.gd` put it. The window drags by its title bar, clamped to the desktop, raises on click, resizes from its bottom-right corner (420x380 minimum; the book fills the body in both axes and existing ink scales with the page) and closes by its close button (as in the prototype: `window_visible` then reads false and nothing reopens it). Inside: the owner's Ragnarok-style chrome (frame slices drawn 1:1 and tiled), the open book, the drawable page interior (`HITBOX_INSET`), tldraw's freehand ink (`freehand.gd`, tldraw 5.3.2 line by line; 4.5 px, `#4465e9`, simulated pressure, bowed toward the spine as the web prototype's displacement filter does; Godot's per-frame mouse-motion merging is turned off so every sample reaches the stroke), one stroke list per spread, finished ink rasterised once into a SubViewport texture, the arrows running the web prototype's 520 ms perspective paper turn (`paper_turn.gd`) with the outgoing half's ink rendered to a SubViewport as the card's face, the spine's multiply gutter, and the pencil cursor over the page (a transparent custom cursor hides the arrow).

Frozen while hidden: the Shell hides the Page (`visible = false`, `process_mode DISABLED`). Measured in the playtest: the desktop's `_process` and `_input` counters stand still, a drag across the hidden page draws nothing and a click on the hidden arrow turns nothing, the finished-ink SubViewport stays at `UPDATE_ONCE` / `UPDATE_DISABLED` and the paper-turn face at `UPDATE_DISABLED`, and the frame's draw calls fall back to the white-page count (40 against 54 with the book shown); on show it resumes with the ink, the spread and the window exactly as left (pixel-identical to before hiding).

## Frozen

`interface.gd`, `errors.gd`, the playtest harness and its verifier. Changing them is an Issue.

## Inside

`desktop.gd` is the desktop and the Tenant node; `sketchbook_window.gd`, `drawing_surface.gd`, `freehand.gd` and `paper_turn.gd` are the prototype's scripts with their global `class_name`s dropped (the module preloads them, so no other module can reach them by name), their pixel paths moved and the corner-resize delta taken in the desktop's pixels. All come from `figma-ui-ux-qwen-pipeline` `prototype/painting-tool-mixbox` @ `7ee5e9c` `viewer-godot/`; `PROVENANCE.md` lists all 20 copied files with hashes and the records their pixels come from. Left behind: the catalogue and viewer windows (`modules/sculpture_viewer`), `paint_palette_prototype.gd`, `assets/paintbox`, `addons/mixbox`, the variant switcher and its Left / Right key cycling, the `?turn-seconds` lever, the perf telemetry and every JavaScriptBridge publish (now `qa_state()` feeding `state()`). The ~40 window-dragging lines are duplicated in `modules/sculpture_viewer/desktop.gd` on purpose (ticket #32; where stacking lives is #24's decision). The desktop sets `texture_filter` linear (the prototype's project default), which its children inherit. `drawing_surface.gd` still sets `Input.use_accumulated_input = false` on the first interactive surface's `_ready`, a global toggle every Tenant then shares (`docs/research/prototype-dependencies.md` flags it; no Tenant so far minds). `face_viewport.msaa_2d = MSAA_4X` is a no-op on GLES3 (a warning on this host, not an error). No project setting changed.

The playtest (`scripts/playtest.sh sketchbook`, on `testing/harness_base.gd`) builds the Shell with this Tenant in the Sketchbook Tab and nothing in the other Tabs, on an X display at 1920x1080, drives it with real mouse events through `Input.parse_input_event` (tab clicks, a nine-sample drag across the left page, the next and previous arrows, a title-bar drag) and reaches the book only through `ShellInterface.tenant_state`; `verify.py` re-hashes the screenshots, re-reads pixels (tldraw's ink blue inside the page rect: none before the drag, ink after, none on spread 2, ink again on spread 1 and after the window moved; the book's page drawn, white outside the window and while hidden) and checks the logged states against the contract and the prototype's numbers: lazy creation, page fill, the desktop 1:1 and centred with the window where the prototype put it, spread 1 blank at launch, one stroke with every sample of the drag, the turn in flight (face SubViewport live, progress between 0 and 1) and landed on a blank spread 2 in no less than the 520 ms, previous back to the ink, the window and its page moved by the title drag, frozen counters, nothing drawn or turned while hidden, quiet SubViewports and draw calls while hidden, identical pixels on resume, the 1440x900 re-fit (35 checks). Evidence of the accepted run is in `docs/evidence/sketchbook/`.

Known gaps: the close button hides the book for good within the session (prototype behaviour); no paintbox and no colour choice (ticket #36); the drawing contract on map #14 (#16) may still change the ink; the rights of the chrome, the page picture and the tldraw port are pending on #35; on this WSL host Godot runs on Mesa's OpenGL ES fallback, so the evidence is software-rendered.
