# sketchbook

## What callers get

`SketchbookInterface.create(deps)` returns a full-rect Control that satisfies the Shell's Tenant contract (`modules/shell/interface.gd`, ticket #24): it lays out from its own `size` / `resized`, never the root viewport, and `state()` is the harness probe. The Shell's demo registry maps `sketchbook` to it. Every file the desktop loads (frame slices, arrows, the page, the palette, the brush, the rest, the brush shader, the Mixbox script) is checked before anything is built; a missing one returns `sketchbook.asset_missing` with the path.

The Page is the painting-tool prototype's desktop at `d2faa30` (tickets #48, #49: the owner put the paintbox, the brush and the holder back), in its variant A ("Sidecar studio", smear A "soft smear"):

- **Paintbox window** (left): the white palette picture with 32 pigment wells in two rows and four trays. A click on a well loads that well's pigment onto the brush (the most saturated pixel of the well). A drag through a tray smears the brush's pigment in soft stamps; where the stamp crosses wet paint the brush picks it up and the carried colour becomes `Mixbox.lerp` of the two (42 % per pick-up), so dragging one pigment through another leaves a third colour on the tray and on the brush. A right click clears a tray. The title bar shows the carried colour. Over the palette the arrow is replaced by the brush cursor.
- **Brush rest** (beneath the palette, inside the paintbox window): the cat holder. While the pointer is over neither the palette nor the page, the brush lies on it, its bristles tinted with the carried pigment (`brush-tip.gdshader`); it leaves the rest when the pointer enters either.
- **Sketchbook window** (right): the owner's Ragnarok-style chrome (slices 1:1, tiled), the open book, tldraw's freehand ink (`freehand.gd`, tldraw 5.3.2 line by line; 4.5 px, bowed toward the spine) drawn in the pigment the brush carries at pen-down, one stroke list per spread, finished ink rasterised once into a SubViewport texture, the arrows running the 520 ms perspective paper turn with the outgoing half's ink as the card's face, the spine's multiply gutter, and the pigment-loaded brush as the cursor over the page. The window resizes from its corner (420x380 minimum) and closes by its close button (prototype behaviour: nothing reopens it).

Both windows drag by their title bars (clamped to the desktop) and raise on click. Mixbox also lerps alpha, and the tray paint it picks up is partly transparent, so a mixed pigment usually carries alpha below 1 and its stroke paints translucent on the page, as in the prototype.

## How it lays out (ticket #63)

The native composition D is 1330x679: variant A's two windows (paintbox 550x575, book 630x545 at the prototype's (170, 345) and (750, 365) on its 1440x972 canvas) moved in to the prototype's own right and bottom margins, so the composition's margin is 60 px on the sides and 52 px top and bottom (the book is 10 px taller than the prototype's so both windows share the bottom edge). From the Tenant's own size S: `s = min(S.x / D.x, S.y / D.y)` scales all art uniformly (no raster art is stretched on one axis); the desktop's logical size becomes S / s, and the leftover E = S / s - D goes entirely to the book window, which grows wider or taller (its pages with it, existing ink scaling with the page). The paintbox keeps 550x575 × s, anchored at the top-left beside the book, its rest beneath the palette. So at every aspect the page is full: the bounding box of the two windows spans the page within 60·s / 52·s on each side. It re-lays out on every `resized`; a window the user dragged or resized keeps that change relative to its slot, clamped inside the desktop. Measured: at 1920x1006 (the page at a 1920x1080 window) and 1920x1000, s = 1.444, paintbox 794x830, book 909 wide; at 1440x820, s = 1.083, paintbox 595x623, book 682x685 (E.y = 78 logical px of extra height).

Frozen while hidden: the Shell hides the Page (`visible = false`, `process_mode DISABLED`); on hide the desktop hands the arrow back (the palette and the page both hide it) and parks the brush. Measured in the playtest: `_process` and `_input` counters stand still, a drag across the hidden page draws nothing and a click on the hidden arrow turns nothing, the finished-ink SubViewport stays at `UPDATE_ONCE` / `UPDATE_DISABLED` and the paper-turn face at `UPDATE_DISABLED`, and the frame's draw calls fall back to the white-page count (47 against 77 with the desktop shown); on show it resumes with the paint, the tray, the carried pigment, the spread and the windows exactly as left (pixel-identical).

## Inside

`desktop.gd` is the desktop and the Tenant node; `paintbox.gd` (`paint_palette_prototype.gd`), `sketchbook_window.gd`, `drawing_surface.gd`, `freehand.gd` and `paper_turn.gd` are the prototype's scripts with their global `class_name`s dropped (the module preloads or loads them, so no other module can reach them by name) and their paths moved; `mixbox/` is Mixbox 2.0 unchanged. All come from `figma-ui-ux-qwen-pipeline` `prototype/painting-tool-mixbox` @ `d2faa30`; `PROVENANCE.md` lists every copied file with its hash, the records its pixels come from, and **the Mixbox licence: CC BY-NC 4.0, non-commercial only, notices kept; ticket #36 decides the road**. Changed from the prototype: the paintbox reads its hover pointer from the last mouse event instead of polling the OS cursor (on the root viewport `get_local_mouse_position` reads the OS pointer, which an event pushed through `Input.parse_input_event` never moves; a real mouse behaves the same); the composition and fill rule above. Left behind: the catalogue and viewer windows (`modules/sculpture_viewer`), the A/B/C variant switcher and its Left / Right key cycling (they would take the arrow keys from every Tab; smear A is fixed), the `?variant` / `?perf` / `?turn-seconds` levers (the performance-route fix of `d2faa30` only made `?perf` show the paintbox, which here is always shown), the perf telemetry, every JavaScriptBridge publish (now `state()`), `addons/stagehand`, and the no-longer-loaded pencil. The ~40 window-dragging lines are duplicated in `modules/sculpture_viewer/desktop.gd` on purpose (ticket #32; where stacking lives is #24's decision). The desktop sets `texture_filter` linear (the prototype's project default), which its children inherit; the paintbox sets nearest for itself as the prototype did. `drawing_surface.gd` still sets `Input.use_accumulated_input = false` on the first interactive surface's `_ready`, a global toggle every Tenant then shares. `face_viewport.msaa_2d = MSAA_4X` is a no-op on GLES3 (a warning on this host, not an error). No project setting changed.

The playtest (`scripts/playtest.sh sketchbook`, on `testing/harness_base.gd`) builds the Shell with this Tenant in the Sketchbook Tab and nothing in the other Tabs, on an X display at 1920x1080, drives it with real mouse events through `Input.parse_input_event` (tab clicks, well clicks, two tray drags, a page drag, the arrows, a title-bar drag, pointer moves) and reaches the desktop only through `ShellInterface.tenant_state`. `verify.py` re-hashes the screenshots, re-reads pixels and checks the logged states: lazy creation and page fill; the fill rule at 1920x1006, 1920x1000 and 1440x820 page sizes (uniform scale, paintbox art at native × s, the book taking the leftover, the windows' bounding box within the native margin by the rects and by the screenshot's non-white pixels); the brush on the rest at launch with a blue tip; a well loading pigment; A smeared, B dragged through it, the carried colour differing from both, and the tray's pixels showing a third colour where B crossed A; one stroke on the page in the carried pigment (by state and by the hue of the painted pixels); the brush off the rest while painting and back on it when idle, its tip in the carried pigment's hue; the turn in flight and landed on a blank spread 2, previous back to the paint; the title drag moving the window and its paint; frozen counters, nothing drawn or turned while hidden, quiet SubViewports and draw calls while hidden, identical pixels on resume, the 1440x900 re-fit and the restore (47 checks). Evidence of the accepted run is in `docs/evidence/sketchbook/`.

Known gaps: Mixbox is CC BY-NC 4.0 and its attribution is not shown in the game (#36); the close button hides the book for good within the session (prototype behaviour); only smear variant A; the drawing contract on map #14 (#16) may still change the ink; the rights of the chrome, the page picture, the brush kit and the tldraw port are pending on #35; the Web export and browser evidence were not re-run for this change; `modules/sculpture_viewer/PROVENANCE.md` still names `7ee5e9c` (#49's other half); on this WSL host Godot runs on Mesa's OpenGL ES fallback, so the evidence is software-rendered.
