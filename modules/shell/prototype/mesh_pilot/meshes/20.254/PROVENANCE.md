# 20.254 Saint Peter

Unknown maker, French. *Saint Peter*, ca. 1106–1112. Limestone with traces of gesso and polychromy. 76.2 × 43.2 × 29.2 cm. RISD Museum 20.254.

The first object made on the Muse-first route; the owner accepted it on 8 October 2026 ("yeah its good enough"). This file is `saint-peter-u10k.glb` from the trial: 10,000 triangles, one 512 px colour texture, no normal map, for drawing unshaded; 324 KB in the pack. Every step, charge and hash is in `modules/shell/prototype/mesh_pilot/saint-peter/PROVENANCE.md`. Views: front and back from the museum's photographs, the left side turned by Muse (no side photograph exists).
