## Geometry, textures and motion

The supplied gameplay screenshot fed the saved Muse editing workflow (`meta/muse-image`, OpenRouter); its output fed fal `meshy/v7.1/image-to-3d`, followed by `fal-ai/meshy/rigging/multi-animation`. The owner authorized a scoped $4 fal pilot. Historical liability is $1.54 ($.02 Muse, $1.20 model, $.32 rig/animation); final provider billing remains unverified. Packaging and sound engineering add $0 and no provider calls.

Blender 4.3.2 with actual Blender MCP scheduling produced the accepted corrected head weights, hand geometry/atlas, source-informed poses and baked animation profiles. Godot 4.7.2 imports the six profiles at 240 FPS without animation optimization. The seven accepted model/hand-atlas hashes and exact source paths are in `provenance.json`; the eight captured audio files have their own source and hash rows. Godot extracts each embedded albedo PNG unchanged when importing the GLB. These generated copies are ignored; the GLB is the canonical input. The precise GLB import receipts are tracked despite the repository's general import-cache ignore. Provider originals, Blender source scenes, experiment receipts and comparisons remain preserved on the prototype branch; this package imports none of them at runtime.

The accepted model is a generated interpretation of the supplied Animal Crossing character; exact original geometry and all hidden details were not recovered. Its uniform shared skeleton, weights, corrected UVs, materials and source bytes are unchanged by packaging. Source scripts were copied with module-local resource paths. The sound changes are separately documented below.

## Sound acquisition and engineering

Original GameCube evidence and mappings are recorded in the dated research note for #235. Original controller and sound dispatch source is ACreTeam/ac-decomp pinned to `09ca8e8b5b24e6ab44047ee980cf0088ad7ecb4c`. [Player sound events](https://github.com/ACreTeam/ac-decomp/blob/09ca8e8b5b24e6ab44047ee980cf0088ad7ecb4c/src/game/m_player_sound.c_inc), [bank variations and gait gain](https://github.com/ACreTeam/ac-decomp/blob/09ca8e8b5b24e6ab44047ee980cf0088ad7ecb4c/src/static/jaudio_NES/game/game64.c_inc).

WDLmaster's [firsthand decoded-sample archive](https://hcs64.com/mboard/forum.php?showthread=64579) contains 1,157 unlabeled samples at 32,000 Hz. The uploader explicitly omitted instrument-specific tuning. Archive SHA-256: `1daba0cc50043bd43dcdadd31a9cd101b9179581a1ab509ed4fdd9a8190f0766`. A sound ID is not a decoded WAV index; matching or recovering bank/sequence metadata is required before claiming a particular WAV is a footstep. Source archive and numerical candidates are research inputs, not runtime dependencies.

Existing event selection preserves terrain bases, distinct DASH offset +40, left +10, occasional variants +20/+30, preceding-special suppression, source gait gain and unity playback pitch. Foot contacts suppress playback in the air, while idle, during blocked motion and during door/dialogue holds. Skid entry, physical launch/landing and door action milestones emit their own cues.

Different streams now play through Godot's native `AudioStreamPolyphonic` with 16 voices. Starting a new footstep or action no longer replaces the stream of the previous cue or cuts its tail. [Native playback API](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_audiostreamplaybackpolyphonic.html).

Default playback now uses four unmodified, 185 ms original-game house footstep recordings from MaloDogfish at [388.893–390.004 seconds](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o7pRJ3Vhu7s&t=388s), plus a 540 ms closing-door capture at 386.490–387.030 seconds. Each file and timestamp is bound in `provenance.json`. The room has an inactive visible radio and stillness roughly 38 dB below the louder step windows. These are AAC-decoded recordings, not recovered isolated soundbank masters. The checkerboard floor identity is unknown. The door capture contains a faint fading background near its start. No denoise, pitch change or individual peak normalization was applied.

The playtest explicitly labels this profile **Sound: original house**. It applies the recorded floor profile uniformly, even when selecting outdoor surfaces; it does not claim recovered grass, wood or other terrain banks. Contact samples alternate and vary using the existing schedule; this assignment does not recover the original captured cue IDs or anatomical side mapping. Actual captured IDs remain unknown (`id=-1`); requested terrain IDs remain separate audit fields. The button cycles House → Stone → adapted surfaces; the final setting preserves the prior authored terrain synthesis for comparison.

Playback begins 15/16 ms into the step recordings at their measured attack, and 24 ms into the door capture, before its 30.4 ms peak. A common step gain offset of 22.853 dB cancels the old -14 dB player attenuation and divides by the source indoor RUN gain (.72), then adds 6 dB common makeup to balance these quiet gameplay recordings against the authored action cues; .54/.72/.9 gait gains scale relatively. Door playback cancels the old -14 dB base, and adds the same 6 dB makeup. All house/door WAV import receipts use lossless PCM (`compress/mode=0`). File bytes and pitch remain unchanged; these level choices do not reconstruct the original engine's master volume or reverb.

The second explicit audition profile, **Sound: original stone**, uses three 185 ms recordings from [EmuRetro's GameCube station escort](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fd0g57lSedA&t=605s), 605.015–605.727 s. Visual contacts remain on stone paving. Their approximately 90 ms bodies and full quiet tails have no sustained music bed; repeated A/C waveforms correlate at .992 across 140 ms. Player/NPC origin, anatomical side and exact bank variation are unassigned. Source code routes NPC and player footsteps through the same material dispatch, with a different NPC gain; that supports the captured stone timbre, not an exact bank-to-WAV reconstruction. Clips are raw AAC-decoded mono PCM with no filtering, individual normalization or pitch change. Source receipts and confidence are in `stone_reference` and the research note.

Stone playback begins 23/21/25 ms into the three files, before each rising attack and with its full peak retained. Its gain offset cancels the old -14 dB base and divides by .8: RUN reproduces the recorded level, and .6/.8/1 gait gains scale relatively. The stone recordings are already louder than the house recordings, so they receive no extra makeup. House and close retain their common +6 dB. Captured profiles are selected explicitly and applied uniformly in the test arena. House floor remains unidentified; stone captures do not supply grass/soil, distinct DASH samples or Nintendo's variant mapping. Actual captured IDs stay -1.

Grass/soil and other terrain banks, distinct DASH samples, skid, jumping/landing, door opening/latch, voice and ambience are not recovered by these captures. The remaining action timbres and the surface-comparison mode use the accepted prototype's deterministic local synthesis, explicitly an adaptation. General-purpose jumping is an extension; source jump/landing event IDs are `0x42A`/`0x42B`, but using those IDs does not prove the added jump matches an original player ability. Music, Animalese, environmental ambience and original room reverb are not included.

Original Nintendo audio, if recovered, is Nintendo-derived and confined to this private educational repository and its private review exports; acquisition is not a redistribution license.

The playtest now dispatches footstep sound and dust at an actual sole lift-to-plant transition, with 50 mm lift / 10 mm plant hysteresis in every gait at unit scale. It no longer assumes half-cycle contacts at phases 0/.5. This follows the museum adapter’s measured contact approach (#236) and leaves all accepted rig, pose, animation and movement data unchanged.

Independent review finds two repeating waveform families across both captures: House b/d and Stone b correlate at .973–.984; House c and Stone a/c at .988–.992. This supports the original captured timbre, but does not recover terrain IDs. These profiles are different recording levels/variants, not evidence of separate banks. House a is 8.7 dB quieter and duller than the common left-side clip; its raw level is deliberately preserved. Stone's contact levels differ by about 1.8 dB. Audition both before selecting a museum mix.
