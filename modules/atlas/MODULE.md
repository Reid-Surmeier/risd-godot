# atlas

## What callers get

`AtlasInterface.create(deps)` returns a full-rect Control that satisfies the Shell's Tenant contract (`modules/shell/interface.gd`, ticket #24): it lays out from its own `size` / `resized`, never the root viewport, and `state()` is the harness probe. The Shell's demo registry maps `map` to it (map #23: the atlas is the first port, proving the seam on the cheapest Tenant). The two data files are checked before anything is built (a missing one returns `atlas.asset_missing` with the path); a missing pixel sheet, panel, font or shader is reported by `load()` as it is reached.

The Page is the atlas's desktop, the prototype's whole desktop as the owner corrected on map #23: the four supplied raster panels — minimap and calendar top-left, the itinerary under it, the chat bottom-left, the notification bubble bottom-right — around the map window, the prototype's frame pixels sliced around a `SubViewportContainer`. The composition is the prototype's 1950x1280 desktop (`PANEL_RECTS`, `FRAME_RECT`) filling the Page (owner correction #63): one uniform scale s = min(page / 1950x1280) for all the raster art, each panel kept at its native distance times s from the page edges nearest its centre (minimap, itinerary and chat to the left, chat and notification to the bottom, notification to the right), and the map window takes the leftover — its left edge beside the left panels and its bottom above the chat keep their native gaps, its top and right run to the page edge minus the desktop's native margin (`FRAME_FAR_GAP`), so it is never smaller than its native size times s and the page is full at any aspect. Every resize lays it out again, panels and window alike. Every window drags: a panel from anywhere on it, the map by its title bar; a press raises the pressed window to the top of the stack; drags clamp to the Page. The map window also resizes by its edges (300 px wide minimum), collapses by its left button and locks by its right one (the lock holds the frame only, as in the prototype). A wheel over a panel is swallowed, so the map beneath does not zoom. Inside, the zoomable atlas is the prototype's `atlas.gd` unchanged in behaviour: drag pans, the wheel zooms at the pointer (x1.18 a notch), double-click zooms in, Home/Esc reset, + and - zoom, arrows pan, F shows the region's full sheet; the HUD picks a region; close-city names appear past 1.8x in PixelMplus (`fonts/LICENSE.txt`), coastline tiles past 1.65x.

Frozen while hidden: the Shell hides the Page (`visible = false`, `process_mode DISABLED`). Measured in the playtest: the desktop's `_process` and `_input` counters stand still, wheel, drag and every key aimed at the hidden map change nothing, panels and stack included, the `SubViewportContainer` flips its SubViewport to `UPDATE_DISABLED` (mode 0) and the frame's draw calls fall back to the white-page count (40 against 65 with the desktop shown); on show it resumes with zoom, camera, window and panels exactly as left (mode 4, pixels identical to before hiding).

## Frozen

`interface.gd`, `errors.gd`, the playtest harness and its verifier. Changing them is an Issue.

## Inside

The compact layout (Page narrower in aspect than the native desktop) uses the accepted
#164 MINI MAP heading over the raster clock. Atlas owns and fits this heading
with its minimap; Shell does not inspect or mutate Atlas children.

`atlas_window.gd` is the desktop and the Tenant node; `atlas.gd` is the map inside its SubViewport (`gui_embed_subwindows`, `UPDATE_ALWAYS` as in the prototype — the container manages it from there). Both come from `qwen-pipeline-experiments` `prototype/atlas-5` @ `421f1cc` (the panels arranged in `08dae0d`, made draggable in `c0b7fc5`, their lettering edited in `421f1cc`); `PROVENANCE.md` lists all 92 copied files with hashes and the ledgers their pixels come from. Left behind: the under-750-px phone layout, the clear-colour override, the JavaScriptBridge publishes (now `snapshot()` feeding `state()`, trimmed to the fields the probe documents), the touch, pinch and trackpad paths (a desktop-only Tenant), the two one-node scenes, `world.png` and the plain geography tiles (never loaded). One prototype bug fixed: a spare `Button.new()` that was never added to the tree leaked at exit. `export_presets.cfg` includes `modules/atlas/*.json` so the Web export carries the data. No project setting changed; the prototype's `gl_compatibility` and `emulate_mouse_from_touch` are not set here (see the open questions on the port's ticket).

The playtest (`scripts/playtest.sh atlas`, on `testing/harness_base.gd`) builds the Shell with the atlas in the Map Tab and nothing in the other Tabs, on an X display at 1920x1080, drives it with real mouse and key events through `Input.parse_input_event` (tab clicks, a panel drag onto the map, a wheel over the panel, wheel, drag-pan, title-bar drag, a drag past the page's corner, an edge resize, the collapse and lock buttons, `+` / Right / F / Home) and reaches the atlas only through `ShellInterface.tenant_state`; `verify.py` re-hashes the screenshots, re-reads pixels (sea and land colours in the map body, the title lettering, each panel's raster art, the moved panel over the map, white above the window, below a resized and below a collapsed window) and checks the logged states against the contract, ticket #30 and the map #23 correction: lazy creation, page fill, the window and the four panels at the fill rule's composition with nothing overlapping and the map on top, the panel drag moving exactly the panel by the drag and raising it, the wheel over it leaving the map's zoom alone, zoom x1.18^3 at the centre, camera moved by drag/zoom, frame moved by the drag and clamped to the page, resized in place with the map body following, collapsed to its title bar and expanded, held under a drag while locked, `+` x1.3, Right 100 world px over the zoom, F to the sheet and back, Home to the fitted world view, frozen counters, unchanged state (panels and stack included) under hidden wheel, drag and keys, update mode and draw calls while hidden, identical pixels on resume, the 1440x900 re-fit of window and panels, and at pages of 1920x1000 and 1440x820 the desktop's bounding box spanning the page on both axes within the native margins with the map window at least native times s (78 checks). Evidence of the accepted run is in `docs/evidence/atlas/`.

Known gaps: the prototype's keys (Home, Esc, arrows, F) are also claimed by other Tenants (map #23, cross-tab shortcuts); GeoNames attribution is not shown on screen; the window frame's and the four panels' rights record is "owner supplied" only; the panels are raster art — nothing behind the chat, calendar or trade chrome.
