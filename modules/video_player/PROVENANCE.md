# Provenance of modules/video_player

Every file below was copied unchanged (the script excepted, see its header) from this repository's
branch `Reid-Surmeier/issue-20-video-player-usability`, commit `f2097ff`, folder
`prototypes/video-player-usability/`, on 2026-09-13. SHA-256 is of the source file at that commit,
which for every non-script file is also the byte-identical copy here. The five preview videos come
from the prototype's Web build stage (`build/web-project/media/`, produced by its `build-web.sh` on
2026-09-08), not from Git. Left behind: `thumbnails/` (five ffmpeg frame grabs the player never
loads — the tiles are cut from the plate), `seedance-motion/verification.json` and
`assets/generation-provenance.json`, `assets/thumbnail-provenance.json` (folded into this file),
`tests/`, `main.tscn`, `project.godot`, `web-export.cfg`, `run.sh`, `build-web.sh`,
`prepare-source.py`, `conform-motion.py`.

Nothing here is hand-drawn. This file folds the prototype's six provenance records:
`assets/generation-provenance.json`, `assets/thumbnail-provenance.json`,
`assets/source-controls/manifest.json`, `assets/seedance-motion/manifest.json`,
`assets/seedance-motion/verification.json` (all under `prototypes/video-player-usability/`) and
`docs/evidence/video-player-usability/source-faithful-v2/generation-provenance.json`, with
`docs/evidence/video-player-usability/media-provenance.json` for the videos — all at `f2097ff`.

**Rights: the five videos are RISD Museum material (owner-authorized Vimeo downloads, issue #18)
and the Fly Through v7 screenshot is an owner-supplied capture of a third-party player window;
their rights record is pending on ticket #35.** They ship here as draft-mockup evidence, exactly as
the other prototypes' pixels do; nothing in this module asserts a licence for them.

## The five videos (`media/`, ticket #31)

The originals are the issue-18 prepared files: 1280x720 (one 1366x694, one 1080x720), 30 fps
Ogg Theora / Vorbis, 409,981,451 bytes together, transcoded by ffmpeg N-126335 from Vimeo's
1280x720 HLS rendition (yt-dlp 2026.08.19, owner-authorized, canonical public page as referrer, no
credential retained). They stay out of Git under `runs/issue-18-vimeo-media/prepared/` (gitignored)
and are verified by hash with `media/prepare.py --check-originals`. The checked-in previews are the
prototype's Web-build transcode of those originals: `-vf scale=480:-2,fps=24 -c:v libtheora -q:v 3
-c:a libvorbis -q:a 1 -ac 2` (`build-web.sh`), 96,946,975 bytes together; `prepare.py --prepare`
rebuilds them and `--check` verifies them. Full detail per video (sizes, codecs, durations, the
ffmpeg arguments, the Vimeo source hashes) is in `media/manifest.json`.
