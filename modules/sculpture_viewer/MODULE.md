# sculpture_viewer

**Setup screen (Issue #110).** The desktop is now the RISD Museum setup screen: a 2540x1680 ground (the approved raster `image-work/paintbox-3d-layout/sculpture-row-risd/review/paintbox-3d-risd-five-rows-text-refined-2x-v12.png` at 1x). The setup window is that raster's left 1050 px (`assets/setup/panel-2x.png`, drawn from 2x pixels with mipmaps), dragged anywhere on it; 18 of its 40 grid cells (`TURNING` in `desktop.gd`, template-matched to the raster with zero error) are `turn_cell.gd` nodes that play a Seedance hover turn from `assets/setup/turn/<name>.png` (eight 216x200 frames per row, frame 0 the approved icon): entering turns forward over 0.7 s and holds, leaving plays back to frame 0. The live viewer window stands exactly over the raster's mock-up viewer (1071.5, 39) at 1357/800 scale. Proof: `playtest/hover_turn.gd` (real pointer on every cell). The frozen harness's layout checks still describe the old catalogue desktop; re-freezing them is its own Issue. The old description below is kept for the viewer's behaviour, which is unchanged.

## What callers get

`SculptureViewerInterface.create(deps)` returns a full-rect Control that satisfies the Shell's Tenant contract (`modules/shell/interface.gd`, ticket #24): it lays out from its own `size` / `resized`, never the root viewport, and `state()` is the harness probe. The Shell's demo registry maps `3d_viewer` to it (map #23; ticket #32 split the Sculpture Viewer pair into two Tenants sharing nothing but the Shell). Every file the desktop and the viewer load is checked before anything is built; a missing one returns `sculpture_viewer.asset_missing` with the path.

The Page is the prototype's desktop, as the owner corrected on map #23 (a Tab shows the whole desktop with all its windows): the 1440x972 white canvas of `references/statue-viewer-desktop` filling the Page (owner correction #63): one uniform scale s = min(page / 1440x972) for all the window art, the desktop's white spanning the whole Page, the catalogue window (the sidebar picture, drag only) at its native (72, 34) by the top-left edges it sits nearest, and the 800x680 viewer window (0.985 in the prototype) given the leftover. The viewer's frame is a raster plate that cannot re-lay out, so it scales uniformly to the largest size that fits its slot — from its native (600, 34) to the page's right and bottom edges less the desktop's native margins (52 and 44) — and centres there: at the owner's wide pages it fills the slot's height and only centres on x (83.5 px of slot either side at a 1920x1000 page, 1.7 px at 1440x820), never smaller than native times s. Every resize lays the desktop out again (a dragged window goes back to its place). Windows drag by their handles (the catalogue anywhere on its picture, the viewer by its top and bottom strips), raise on click and stack in click order, clamped to the desktop, as the prototype's `desktop.gd` does. Inside the viewer: the clean media-player plate with its baked controls erased by `player_base.gdshader`, a 529x486 SubViewport with the Buddha scan under a key light with one orthogonal shadow and a fill light on a studio floor; drag orbits (0.35 deg per viewer px in yaw, 0.25 in pitch, clamped -55..45), the wheel moves the camera 0.45 per notch (2.8..10.5; closer inspection per #82), double-click resets, previous / next step 30 degrees, play / pause runs the 8 deg/s autoplay that also drives the scrubber, the scrubber sets the yaw, audio and menu toggle their state; every control plays its Muse + Seedance motion frames (hover, press, release). The keys of the prototype (arrows, Space, M, Home, Esc) still work while the viewer has focus.

Frozen while hidden: the Shell hides the Page (`visible = false`, `process_mode DISABLED`). Measured in the playtest: the desktop's `_process` and `_input` counters stand still, wheel, drag and a button click aimed at the hidden viewer change nothing, the `SubViewportContainer` flips the 3D SubViewport to `UPDATE_DISABLED` (mode 0) and the frame's draw calls fall back to the white-page count (40 against 60 with the viewer shown); on show it resumes with the camera, the windows and their order exactly as left (pixel-identical to before hiding).

## Inside

`desktop.gd` is the desktop and the Tenant node; `viewer.gd` is the prototype's `main.gd` inside the viewer window. Both come from `figma-ui-ux-qwen-pipeline` `prototype/painting-tool-mixbox` @ `7ee5e9c` `viewer-godot/`; `PROVENANCE.md` lists all 18 copied files with hashes and the records their pixels come from. Left behind: the sketchbook window (`modules/sketchbook`), the Mixbox paintbox and its variant switcher, the `?qa-viewer` isolation, the `?qa-plate` test hook, the `?lod-bias` / `?no-shadow` levers, the perf telemetry and every JavaScriptBridge publish (now `qa_state()` feeding `state()`, trimmed to the fields the probe documents), the two one-node scenes. The viewer's SubViewport keeps the prototype's `UPDATE_ONCE`, re-armed on every camera change (and every other frame while the autoplay orbit runs); after a hide and show the container sets it to `UPDATE_ALWAYS` until the next camera change re-arms it. The desktop sets `texture_filter` linear (the prototype's project default; this project's is nearest), which its children inherit. `3124123123.jpg.import` is force-tracked (`*.import` is otherwise ignored here) for the one setting the prototype relied on: the 6568x6192 texture imported at 2048 px. No project setting changed; the prototype's `gl_compatibility`, `canvas_items` stretch and `emulate_mouse_from_touch` are not set here.

The playtest (`scripts/playtest.sh sculpture_viewer`, on `testing/harness_base.gd`) builds the Shell with this Tenant in the 3D Viewer Tab and nothing in the other Tabs, on an X display at 1920x1080, drives it with real mouse events through `Input.parse_input_event` (tab clicks, the play/pause button, a drag-orbit, two wheel notches, the next button, a drag on the viewer's top strip, a drag on the catalogue, a raising click) and reaches the viewer only through `ShellInterface.tenant_state`; `verify.py` re-hashes the screenshots, re-reads pixels (the sculpture in the viewport, the catalogue picture, white outside the windows and while hidden, the paused render standing still, orbit and zoom changing it) and checks the logged states against the contract and the prototype's numbers: lazy creation, page fill, the desktop at the fill rule's uniform scale with both windows where the rule puts them, autoplay while shown, pause, yaw and pitch by the drag over the pointer scale, distance by the wheel, 30 degrees per next, the window moved by the strip drag, the catalogue raised and moved, the viewer raised by a click, frozen counters, unchanged state under hidden wheel, drag and click, update mode and draw calls while hidden, identical pixels on resume, the 1440x900 re-fit, and at pages of 1920x1000 and 1440x820 the plate at least native times s, spanning its slot and the page (within the native margins) on its limiting axis and centred in its slot on the other (52 checks). Evidence of the accepted run is in `docs/evidence/sculpture-viewer/`.

Known gaps: the prototype's keys (arrows, Space, M, Home, Esc) are also claimed by other Tenants (map #23, cross-tab shortcuts); the menu button only closes a menu that never opens (as in the prototype); the timer reads a fixed "04:21"; the rights of the Buddha scan, the plate and the catalogue picture are pending on #35; on this WSL host Godot runs on Mesa's OpenGL ES fallback, so the evidence is software-rendered.
