# sculpture_viewer

## What callers get

`SculptureViewerInterface.create(deps)` returns a full-rect Control that satisfies the Shell's Tenant contract (`modules/shell/interface.gd`, ticket #24): it lays out from its own `size` / `resized`, never the root viewport, and `state()` is the harness probe. The Shell's demo registry maps `3d_viewer` to it (map #23; ticket #32 split the Sculpture Viewer pair into two Tenants sharing nothing but the Shell). Every file the desktop and the viewer load is checked before anything is built; a missing one returns `sculpture_viewer.asset_missing` with the path.

The Page is the prototype's desktop, as the owner corrected on map #23 (a Tab shows the whole desktop with all its windows): the 1440x972 white canvas kept 1:1 and centred where it fits, shrunk to fit a smaller Page, with the catalogue window (the sidebar picture, drag only) at (72, 34) and the 800x680 viewer window at (600, 34) scaled 0.985, exactly `references/statue-viewer-desktop`. Windows drag by their handles (the catalogue anywhere on its picture, the viewer by its top and bottom strips), raise on click and stack in click order, clamped to the desktop, as the prototype's `desktop.gd` does. Inside the viewer: the clean media-player plate with its baked controls erased by `player_base.gdshader`, a 529x486 SubViewport with the Buddha scan under a key light with one orthogonal shadow and a fill light on a studio floor; drag orbits (0.35 deg per viewer px in yaw, 0.25 in pitch, clamped -55..45), the wheel moves the camera 0.45 per notch (4.7..10.5), double-click resets, previous / next step 30 degrees, play / pause runs the 8 deg/s autoplay that also drives the scrubber, the scrubber sets the yaw, audio and menu toggle their state; every control plays its Muse + Seedance motion frames (hover, press, release). The keys of the prototype (arrows, Space, M, Home, Esc) still work while the viewer has focus.

Frozen while hidden: the Shell hides the Page (`visible = false`, `process_mode DISABLED`). Measured in the playtest: the desktop's `_process` and `_input` counters stand still, wheel, drag and a button click aimed at the hidden viewer change nothing, the `SubViewportContainer` flips the 3D SubViewport to `UPDATE_DISABLED` (mode 0) and the frame's draw calls fall back to the white-page count (40 against 60 with the viewer shown); on show it resumes with the camera, the windows and their order exactly as left (pixel-identical to before hiding).

## Frozen

`interface.gd`, `errors.gd`, the playtest harness and its verifier. Changing them is an Issue.

## Inside

`desktop.gd` is the desktop and the Tenant node; `viewer.gd` is the prototype's `main.gd` inside the viewer window. Both come from `figma-ui-ux-qwen-pipeline` `prototype/painting-tool-mixbox` @ `7ee5e9c` `viewer-godot/`; `PROVENANCE.md` lists all 18 copied files with hashes and the records their pixels come from. Left behind: the sketchbook window (`modules/sketchbook`), the Mixbox paintbox and its variant switcher, the `?qa-viewer` isolation, the `?qa-plate` test hook, the `?lod-bias` / `?no-shadow` levers, the perf telemetry and every JavaScriptBridge publish (now `qa_state()` feeding `state()`, trimmed to the fields the probe documents), the two one-node scenes. The viewer's SubViewport keeps the prototype's `UPDATE_ONCE`, re-armed on every camera change (and every other frame while the autoplay orbit runs); after a hide and show the container sets it to `UPDATE_ALWAYS` until the next camera change re-arms it. The desktop sets `texture_filter` linear (the prototype's project default; this project's is nearest), which its children inherit. `3124123123.jpg.import` is force-tracked (`*.import` is otherwise ignored here) for the one setting the prototype relied on: the 6568x6192 texture imported at 2048 px. No project setting changed; the prototype's `gl_compatibility`, `canvas_items` stretch and `emulate_mouse_from_touch` are not set here.

The playtest (`scripts/playtest.sh sculpture_viewer`, on `testing/harness_base.gd`) builds the Shell with this Tenant in the 3D Viewer Tab and nothing in the other Tabs, on an X display at 1920x1080, drives it with real mouse events through `Input.parse_input_event` (tab clicks, the play/pause button, a drag-orbit, two wheel notches, the next button, a drag on the viewer's top strip, a drag on the catalogue, a raising click) and reaches the viewer only through `ShellInterface.tenant_state`; `verify.py` re-hashes the screenshots, re-reads pixels (the sculpture in the viewport, the catalogue picture, white outside the windows and while hidden, the paused render standing still, orbit and zoom changing it) and checks the logged states against the contract and the prototype's numbers: lazy creation, page fill, the desktop 1:1 and centred with both windows where the prototype put them, autoplay while shown, pause, yaw and pitch by the drag over the pointer scale, distance by the wheel, 30 degrees per next, the window moved by the strip drag, the catalogue raised and moved, the viewer raised by a click, frozen counters, unchanged state under hidden wheel, drag and click, update mode and draw calls while hidden, identical pixels on resume, the 1440x900 re-fit (43 checks). Evidence of the accepted run is in `docs/evidence/sculpture-viewer/`.

Known gaps: the prototype's keys (arrows, Space, M, Home, Esc) are also claimed by other Tenants (map #23, cross-tab shortcuts); the menu button only closes a menu that never opens (as in the prototype); the timer reads a fixed "04:21"; the rights of the Buddha scan, the plate and the catalogue picture are pending on #35; on this WSL host Godot runs on Mesa's OpenGL ES fallback, so the evidence is software-rendered.
